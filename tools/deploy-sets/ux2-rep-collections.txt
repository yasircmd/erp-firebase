# (owner, 2026-10-25/26; docs/modules/REP_COLLECTIONS.md, Q294-Q298, Q309-Q310).
# EVERY function: glWriting.js (the new RepReceipt voucher type) is required
# by nearly every function, and partyLedger.js (the Sales Rep party) by all
# the posting ones. Four functions are new (deploy-functions.sh binds their
# invokers): saveSalesRepSettings, getRepReceipts, cancelRepReceipt,
# receivePdcsFromRep. Run WITH --screens (Sales Invoice Configuration's
# Sales reps, CBT and JV's Sales rep party, PDC Received / Issued), then the
# "hosting" task (the Employee Portal's collections and receipts, the Help).
# No rules change.
# After it: in Sales Invoice Configuration → Sales reps, choose each
# company's Rep Cash Clearing account (a new postable account, e.g. under
# current assets) and check each rep's code. From the deploy, a rep's
# collection posts at once; their drafts from before stay drafts for the
# office to post as before.
