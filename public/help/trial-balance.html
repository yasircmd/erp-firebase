Trial Balance

Every account's opening balance, movements and closing balance for a period, with drill-down to the ledger.

Screen: Trial Balance (TB)

What it is for

The Trial Balance lists every account with its Opening balance, the period's Transactions (debit and credit) and its Closing balance. Total debits equal total credits; it is the first check that the books are complete.

Step by step

  1. Open Trial Balance (TB).
  2. Choose the Company and the period. From starts at the beginning of the company's financial year; change it as needed.
  3. Narrow by Division, Branch, Cost Center or Profit Center if needed.
  4. Select Run Report.
  5. Select an account to open its General Ledger for the same period; Back returns here.
  6. Copy Table to Excel, or Print.

Before year-end closing

After a financial year is closed, its income and expense accounts are zeroed into Retained Earnings by the year-end closing entry. Tick Before year-end closing to see the closed year's income and expense balances as they were.

Good to know

  • Anything dated before From is in the Opening column.
  • Project shows only the lines of one project (or those with **No project**).
  • See Period Closing for the year-end entry.
