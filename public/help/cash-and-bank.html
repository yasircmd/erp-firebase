Cash and Bank Transactions (CBT)

Payments, receipts and transfers through cash and bank accounts, with vendor and customer allocation.

Screen: Cash and Bank Transactions (CBT)

What it is for

Records every movement through a cash or bank account:

TypeUse it for
Bank Payment / Cash PaymentPaying a vendor, an expense, an advance
Bank Receipt / Cash ReceiptMoney received from a customer or anyone else
Fund TransferMoving money between your own cash and bank accounts

Before you start

  • Your cash accounts (codes starting 10) and bank accounts (codes starting 11) in the Chart of Accounts.
  • Vendors and customers set up with their payables / receivables account.

Step by step

  1. Open Cash and Bank Transactions and select New Transaction.
  2. Choose the Transaction Type, then the Company.
  3. Choose the Ledger Account: the cash or bank account the money moves through (for a Fund Transfer, the account it moves from).
  4. Fill the date, description and reference.
  5. Add the other side, one line per account: who it was Paid To, Received From, or for a transfer the account it goes To; the Amount, a description, and a cost center where the account needs one.
  6. For a vendor or customer, pick the Vendor / Customer and Allocate the amount to their bills (see Paying and allocating).
  7. Save as Draft, or post. Where an approval applies to that transaction type, it goes for approval instead.
  8. Print gives the payment or receipt voucher.

Correcting a posted transaction

Select Reverse Entry and choose the date: a mirror entry is posted and any bills it settled become unpaid again. Then enter it correctly.

What happens in the books

  • Payment: Dr each line's account (e.g. the vendor's payables), Cr the cash or bank account.
  • Receipt: Dr the cash or bank account, Cr each line's account (e.g. the customer's receivables).
  • Fund Transfer: Dr the account it goes to, Cr the account it comes from.

The entry gets a Voucher No. per type and company, and a Txn Ref.

Rights

As Journal Entries: view, draft and post are separate rights. Approval can be set per transaction type (Approval Configuration).

Questions and mistakes

  • "…is closed for …": the date is in a closed month (Period Closing).
  • The allocation doesn't fit: a bill was paid by someone else in the meantime; the ERP checks again when posting. Open Allocate and choose again.
  • Post-dated cheques are not entered here: use Post-dated Cheques.
