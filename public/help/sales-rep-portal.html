Sales reps in the Employee Portal

Sales reps see their customers, send orders and record collections from the Employee Portal on their phone.

What it is for

A sales rep uses the Employee Portal on their phone. Its Sales tab shows the rep's customers with each one's balance and overdue amount, and lets the rep send an order and record a collection. Nothing the rep does posts by itself: the office confirms orders and posts collections in the ERP, with every check the ERP makes.

Before you start

  • The rep is an employee with portal access (Employees → portal), and has a cost center on their employee record.
  • In Sales Invoice Configuration → **Sales reps**, the office adds the rep, their customers and, for cash collections, the rep's cash account. A Sales manager sees all the company's customers.
  • The customers have a price list (Price Listing, by their classification).

Step by step

  1. Open the portal and the Sales tab.
  2. New order on a customer: choose items (at the customer's price list price; the stock free to sell is shown), quantities and the warehouse, then Send the order to the office. It is a draft Sales Order the office confirms; the stock is reserved then.
  3. Collect on a customer: choose Cash or Cheque and the amount. A cheque takes its number, date, bank and a photo. Cash becomes a draft Cash Receipt to the rep's cash account, a cheque a draft PDC Received; the office posts them.
  4. My orders and collections shows each one's status: *With the office* until it is confirmed or posted.

Good to know

  • The portal needs a connection: nothing is kept on the phone.
  • Prices come from the price list only; the rep can't change them.
  • For example: Sam Seller sends an order of 2 × RM-001 at 60.00 for Corner Shop; the office confirms it in Sales Order.
