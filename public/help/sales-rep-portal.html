Sales reps in the Employee Portal

Sales reps see their customers, send orders, collect cash and cheques with a receipt at once, and see what they hold, from the Employee Portal on their phone.

What it is for

A sales rep uses the Employee Portal on their phone. Its Sales tab shows the rep's customers with each one's balance and overdue amount, and lets the rep send an order and collect a payment. An order is a draft the office confirms. A collection is final at once: the customer gets its receipt straight away, and the rep holds the cash or the cheque until they hand it to the office.

Before you start

  • The rep is an employee with portal access (Employees → portal), and has a cost center on their employee record.
  • In Sales Invoice Configuration → Sales reps, the office chooses the company's Rep Cash Clearing account, adds the rep with their code (in their receipt numbers) and their customers. A Sales manager sees all the company's customers.
  • The customers have a price list (Price Listing, by their classification).

Step by step

  1. Open the portal and the Sales tab.
  2. New order on a customer: choose items (at the customer's price list price; the stock free to sell is shown), quantities and the warehouse, then Send the order to the office. It is a draft Sales Order the office confirms; the stock is reserved then.
  3. Collect on a customer: choose Cash or Cheque and the amount. A cheque takes its number, date, bank and a photo.
  4. What it pays: the customer's open invoices are listed, the oldest first. Tick the ones the payment settles (the amount fills in, up to what each invoice owes); what is left goes on account, for the office to match later.
  5. Record and give the receipt. The receipt opens: its number, the customer, the amount in figures and words, cash or the cheque, and the invoices it pays. Share sends it as a PDF through the phone's own share sheet (WhatsApp, email …); Download PDF saves it.
  6. I hold, at the top of the Sales tab, shows the cash and the cheques the rep still holds. My receipts lists every receipt with what it is now, and opens one to share it again.

Your receipts and what you hold

  • Each rep numbers their own receipts, RC-<code>-000001, then 000002 and so on, cash and cheques together. A number is never used twice.
  • Cash stays on the rep until the cashier takes it: they enter a Cash Receipt in Cash & Bank, choose the rep and match the rep's receipts. The receipt then shows Handed over (or Part handed over), and I hold drops by itself.
  • A cheque is a post-dated cheque at once, with the rep until the office ticks it received from the rep in PDC Received. It then shows Handed over, later Cleared (or Bounced).
  • A wrong receipt (a wrong amount or customer) is cancelled by the office only, with the reason: it shows Cancelled, and the rep gives a new receipt.

What happens in the books

  • Cash: Dr Rep Cash Clearing (against the rep) · Cr the customer, at once. The cashier's Cash Receipt then moves it: Dr Cash · Cr Rep Cash Clearing.
  • A cheque: Dr PDC Received Clearing · Cr the customer, at once, as any PDC Received; it clears to the bank on its date.
  • Either way the customer owes less from the moment the receipt is given.

Good to know

  • The portal needs a connection: nothing is kept on the phone.
  • Prices come from the price list only; the rep can't change them.
  • A customer invoiced in a foreign currency is not collected from the portal: the office records its receipts.
  • For example: Sam Seller (code SS) collects 350.00 in cash from Corner Shop, ticking their invoice of 300.00; 50.00 goes on account. The receipt RC-SS-000001 is shared by WhatsApp; Sam holds 350.00 until the cashier takes it.
