Recurring Journal Entries

Entries that repeat on a schedule — rent, depreciation-like accruals, prepaid expenses spread over months.

Screen: Recurring Journal Entries

What it is for

A recurring entry is a template that posts the same journal on a schedule: monthly rent accrual, a prepaid insurance spread over twelve months, a monthly provision. Each run becomes a normal journal entry dated on its run date.

Before you start

The accounts exist, as for a Journal Entry.

Step by step

  1. Open Recurring Journal Entries and select New Recurring Entry.
  2. Fill the header and lines as on a JV. The description may include {MONTH}, {DATE} or {YEAR} (for example "Rent {MONTH}" posts "Rent Oct-2026").
  3. Choose the Amounts:
    • Fixed: the lines' amounts on every run;
    • Spread a total: a total shared equally over the runs;
    • Spread by service period: a total over a service period (from, to), each month charged for its days; for example 12,000 for 16 Sep – 15 Sep gives 500, then 1,000 a month, then 500.
  4. Choose the Schedule: monthly on a day (a day past the month's end runs on its last day), monthly at month end, weekly on a weekday, or monthly on the first … last weekday; every 1, 2, 3… months or weeks (3 = quarterly, 12 = yearly).
  5. Choose when it Ends: on an end date, or after a number of runs.
  6. Check the preview: every run date with its amount.
  7. Save, then activate it (with approval, where set up).

Posting the runs

Runs are posted by a person, not automatically. When a run's date comes, Recurring Journal Entries Due to Post appears in Home tasks. Select Post Due Runs (on the list for all, or on one entry). Missed past dates are posted each with its own date.

Run by run

On an entry's run list:

  • Skip a date (no posting that month);
  • Reverse a posted run (a mirror entry; then Post again or Skip);
  • a run that failed (an account made inactive, a closed month…) shows the reason; fix it and post again.

Changing an active entry

Pause, edit, then resume (it goes through approval again where set up). End stops it for good.

What happens in the books

Each run posts a journal entry (numbered RJV-…) dated on its run date, seen in the General Ledger like any JV. Reverse runs from this screen, not from the JV screen.

Rights

As Journal Entries; activating, pausing, ending, skipping and reversing need the post right.
