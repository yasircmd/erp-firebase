Getting started

Signing in, the menu, Home tasks, favourites, and signing out.

Signing in

  1. Open the ERP's address in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  2. On Sign In, enter:
    • Corporate ID: your organisation's ID, given by your administrator;
    • Email address and Password: your own ERP account.
  3. Select Sign In. You arrive at the menu (the Module Hierarchy).

If the ERP says your account is inactive or not configured, ask your administrator: an account must be active and assigned to at least one company and role.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, open Forgot Password, enter your email address and select Send Reset Link. A link to set a new password arrives by email. Once signed in, you can change your password at any time from the user menu (your name, top right) → Change Password.

The menu

The menu is a tree of areas (Finance, Procurement, Inventory and so on). You see only the screens your role allows.

  • Select an area to open it; select a screen to start working in it.
  • Search modules (top bar): type part of a screen's name to filter the menu. Enter opens the screen when only one matches; Esc clears the search.
  • The double-arrow button expands or collapses the whole tree.
  • Inside a screen, Back to Hierarchy returns to the menu. If you have unsaved changes, the ERP asks before leaving.

Favourites

Put the screens you use most at the top of the menu: user menu → Configure Favourites, select the star next to each screen you want, then Done.

Home tasks

The Tasks button (top bar) shows what is waiting for you, with a count. Select a line to open that document. What you may see, depending on your role:

BlockWhat it means
Your Pending ApprovalsDocuments waiting for your approval.
Rejected — Needs Your ResubmissionYour documents an approver rejected, with the reason. Correct and submit again.
Ready for You to Act OnApproved documents your team follows up (for example, raising the purchase order for an approved requisition). The teams are set in Approval Configuration.
My Drafts — Not Yet Submitted or PostedDocuments you saved as drafts.
Post-dated Cheques Due to ClearCheques whose date has come.
Recurring Journal Entries Due to PostRecurring entries whose date has come.
Company / Employee Documents ExpiringLicences, contracts or employee documents near or past expiry.
Employee Requests Waiting for YouEmployees' queries, loan requests and claims to handle.

How approvals work is explained in Approvals.

The user menu

Select your name (top right) for:

  • My Portal: your own leave, payslips and requests, when your ERP user is linked to an employee record;
  • Light mode / Dark mode;
  • Configure Favourites;
  • Refresh: reloads the menu (after an administrator changed it or your rights);
  • Change Password;
  • Log Out.

Help

The Help button (top bar) opens this help in a new tab. Inside a screen, it opens that screen's page directly.

Signing out, and the automatic sign-out

Use Log Out in the user menu when you finish, especially on a shared computer. After 10 minutes without activity the ERP warns you, and signs you out 30 seconds later unless you select Stay Signed In. Anything not saved is lost, so save drafts as you go.
