Getting started

Signing in, the menu, Home tasks, favourites, and signing out.

Signing in

  1. Open the ERP's address in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  2. On Sign In, enter:
    • Corporate ID: your organisation's ID, given by your administrator;
    • Email, user ID or Employee Code and Password: your own ERP account. The user ID is your Username in User Management; your email works as before. An employee with an Employee Portal account can sign in here too, with the Employee Code.
  3. Select Sign In. You arrive at Home, the menu of modules.

The Corporate ID decides which organisation's ERP you sign in to. The field starts empty; your browser can save it with your sign-in and offer it next time.

One sign-in for everyone. Someone with no ERP access (an employee, or a sales rep who uses only the portal) is taken straight on to the Employee Portal, already signed in. Someone with both lands on Home, with My Portal in the user menu as before. The portal's own address still works: it asks for the same Corporate ID, then the Employee Code and password.

If two users were given the same user ID before user IDs had to be unique, they sign in with their email until one is renamed.

If the ERP says your account is inactive or not configured, ask your administrator: an account must be active and assigned to at least one company and role.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, open Forgot Password, enter your Corporate ID and email address and select Send Reset Link. A link to set a new password arrives by email. The link works once and for an hour: if it has expired, ask for a new one the same way. Once signed in, you can change your password at any time from the user menu (your name, top right) → Change Password.

The menu

The menu is a tree of areas (Finance, Procurement, Inventory and so on). You see only the screens your role allows.

The title above the menu names your company: your only company, or else the main one (reference code 01) of the companies you are assigned. When a company is assigned to you, it shows as soon as you come back to Home.

Choosing your companies

With more than one company, the button after the title (All companies at first) chooses which of them you work in. Tick the companies and select Apply:

  • The screens offer only the companies you chose in their company lists, and with one chosen they fill it in for you, as for a user with only one company. The title then names it.
  • Everywhere: Home tasks list only the chosen companies' documents (a task with no company always shows); My dashboard, the MIS Dashboard and MIS Reports offer only the chosen companies; and Master Data Import refuses a row for a company you did not choose.
  • It's a filter, not a change of what you may do: every company is still yours, and Select all brings them all back. A document already saved for another company still shows its company.
  • Your choice is kept for the next time you sign in, on any computer.

For example, an accountant assigned Example Trading and Example Services who works on Example Services all week chooses it alone, and no longer picks the company on each entry.

  • Select an area to open it; select a screen to start working in it.
  • Search modules (top bar): type part of a screen's name to filter the menu. Enter opens the screen when only one matches; Esc clears the search.
  • Short codes: each screen has a code of 2 to 4 letters, shown beside it in the menu (for example COA for Chart of Accounts, JV for Journal Entries, GRN for Goods Receipt Note, PO for Purchase Order). Type the code and press Enter to open that screen at once, however many others match. The codes are the same for everyone.
  • The double-arrow button expands or collapses the whole tree.
  • Without the mouse: F2 goes to Search modules. ↓ moves to the first row shown, and ↑ ↓ move through the menu. → opens an area or folder and ← closes it. Enter opens a screen. Tab reaches the same rows, each outlined when it has the focus.
  • Inside a screen, ← Home (top left) returns to the menu. If you have unsaved changes, the ERP asks before leaving. Enter and Esc also work on every screen: see Working with screens.

Favourites

Put the screens you use most at the top of the menu: user menu → Configure Favourites, select the star next to each screen you want, then Done. Until you choose your own, you see your role's defaults, if your administrator has set some (Roles & Permissions).

Your dashboard on Home

If you use the MIS Dashboard, you can show your tiles on Home as well: tick Show on Home on the MIS Dashboard screen. My dashboard then sits at the top of Home, with its company and Open MIS Dashboard. It is off unless you choose it.

The user menu

Select your name (top right) for:

  • My Portal: your own leave, payslips and requests, when your ERP user is linked to an employee record;
  • Light mode / Dark mode: your choice is kept with you, on every computer you sign in on. The ERP starts in light mode until you choose;
  • Configure Favourites;
  • Refresh: reloads the menu (after an administrator changed it or your rights);
  • Email settings: when your tasks are emailed to you (see Task emails);
  • Change Password;
  • Log Out.

Task emails

The ERP can email you your tasks, at the address you sign in with. Choose how in Email settings (your name, top right):

ChoiceWhat you get
Each task as it reaches meAn email within a few minutes when a document waits for your approval, or when one of yours is rejected; and the summary.
Approvals at once, the rest in a summaryThe default: approvals within a few minutes; everything else in the summary.
A summary onlyOne email listing your open tasks.
No emailsNothing.

The summary lists every open task of yours, by kind, as Home's Tasks shows them: approvals, your rejected items, follow-ups, cheques due, recurring entries due, CRM activities, documents expiring, employee requests, clearance steps and leave returns to record. Choose its time (on the hour or half hour, in the company's time, Asia/Dubai) and its days. With no days ticked there is no summary; none is sent when nothing is open.

An email says what is waiting (for example Purchase Order PO-26-01-000012, 4,250.00, waiting for your approval) and links to the ERP: sign in and open it from Tasks. It carries no other figures and no attachments. Do not reply to it: the mailbox that sends it is not watched for replies.

Employees using the Employee Portal are emailed when their request or leave changes (approved, rejected, answered or resolved), at their work email (else their personal email). They switch it off on the portal's Me page: Email me my requests' updates.

Emails start once your administrator has set up the sending mailbox, in Company Master: your company's own, or your corporate's.

Help

The Help button (top bar), or F1, opens this help in a new tab. Inside a screen, it opens that screen's page directly. Help uses the ERP's light or dark theme; its Light mode / Dark mode button switches both.

Back to the ERP (top of every Help page) closes Help and returns you to the ERP where you were; in the installed app, where Help has no back button of its own, this is the way back. On a phone, each page starts with its own text; All pages jumps to the list of every page, below.

Signing out, and the automatic sign-out

Use Log Out in the user menu when you finish, especially on a shared computer. After 30 minutes without activity the ERP warns you, and signs you out 30 seconds later unless you select Stay Signed In. Anything not saved is lost, so save drafts as you go.

Installing the app

The ERP and the Employee Portal can each be installed as an app, with its own icon (ERP and Employee Portal) and its own window.

  • On a computer (Chrome or Edge): open the ERP, then click the Install icon at the right of the address bar.
  • On an Android phone: open the page in Chrome, then use the menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • On an iPhone or iPad: open the page in Safari, then use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Once installed, the app opens quickly: its pages and styles are kept on the device, and a newer version is fetched each time you open it. You still sign in as usual. Everything you open or save goes to the server, so the app needs a connection: without one it says You're offline, and nothing is saved.

On a phone

The ERP works on a phone for looking things up and approving; data entry is for a computer.

  • Home fits the phone's screen: the menu, the search and your tasks.
  • Approvals open as a compact card, where you approve or reject (Approvals).
  • These reports fit the phone's screen:
    • Trial Balance and the aging reports (Customer and Vendor Aging, Inventory Aging) keep their first column fixed; swipe the table sideways for the other figures;
    • Stock Availability and the statements of account show each row as a card, each figure with its heading.
    • MIS Reports shows each line as a card, the chart above; the MIS Dashboard shows its tiles one under another.

Their filters wrap to the phone's width. Copy to Excel and Print are better done on a computer.

  • Every other screen opens at a computer's width, as before: pinch to zoom. A notice says This screen is best on a computer; close it with × and it stays closed until you close the ERP's tab or app.

Nothing changes on a computer or a tablet.
