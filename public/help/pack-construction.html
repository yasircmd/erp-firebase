Construction pack

Contracts with their bill of quantities, progress claims with retention and advance recovery, retention releases, subcontracts and their claims, site reports.

What it is for

The Construction pack runs contracts on your projects, from the bill of quantities to the monthly claims. Its screens are in the Construction folder.

ScreenWhat it holds
ContractsA contract with a customer on a project: its value, retention % and advance recovery %.
Bill of quantitiesEach BOQ item of a contract: its section, unit, quantity and rate; Amount is worked out.
Progress claimsA month's claim: for each BOQ item the quantity done to date and its rate.
Advances billedAn advance billed to the customer.
Retention releasesRetention released to be paid.
SubcontractsWork given to a subcontractor on a project.
Subcontractor claimsA subcontractor's claim, its retention held.
Site reportsThe day on site: workers, hours (Man-hours worked out), equipment, progress and issues.

How a progress claim is worked out

For each line: Value to date = quantity to date × rate; Claimed before = what the contract's earlier claims claimed for the same BOQ item (a cancelled claim not counted); This claim = value to date − claimed before. Then for the claim:

  • This claim = its lines' This claim, plus any variations;
  • Retention held = retention % of this claim;
  • Advance recovered = advance recovery % of this claim;
  • Net due = this claim − retention − advance recovered;
  • Claimed before and Claimed to date for the whole contract.

A claim is worked out when it is saved. If an earlier claim is changed, save the later ones again.

To the books

A customer invoice can't have a line below zero, so a Certified claim goes to the accounts in three steps, from its To the books panel:

  1. Invoice the claim: a customer invoice for This claim, on the contract's project.
  2. Hold retention: a journal moving the retention from the customer to Retention receivable.
  3. Recover advance: a journal moving the advance recovered from the customer to Advances received.

The customer then owes the net due, as the claim shows. Advances billed puts an advance on the customer, and Retention releases moves retention back to the customer when it is due. A Subcontractor claim makes the subcontractor's vendor invoice on the project, then a journal holding its retention.

Before you start

  • Each contract's project in Projects.
  • The Setup of Progress claims, Advances billed, Retention releases and Subcontractor claims: the sales type and accounts (Pack setup lists them).
  • Your contract revenue's and subcontract costs' tax codes in Tax Configuration.

Reports

The pack adds Claimed by contract, Retention held by customer, Work done by BOQ item, Subcontractor claims by project and Man-hours by project to your MIS reports.
