Item Sub Category

Group items by type or line so stock and reporting match the company’s product structure.

Screen: Item Sub-Category

What it is for

Item Sub Category groups items into categories such as product line, department or stock family. This makes reporting and planning easier.

Before you start

  • The parent item categories are known.
  • Staff agree which grouping is used for reporting and stock control.

Step by step

  1. Open Item Sub Category.
  2. Add a group or update an existing category.
  3. Save and link items to the correct category.

What happens in the books

This screen does not create a ledger entry. It provides classification used by stock reporting and item setup.

Rights

Usually limited to stock setup or admin roles.

Questions and mistakes

  • If a category is missing from the list, it has not been saved or mapped for the selected company.
