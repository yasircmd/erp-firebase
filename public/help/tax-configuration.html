Tax Configuration

Each company's sales tax: UAE VAT, India GST or a tax of its own, with its components, accounts and tax codes rated by date.

Screen: Tax Configuration

What it is for

Tax Configuration says how a company charges sales tax: its regime (UAE VAT, India GST, or a tax of its own), the components the tax is charged as, each posted to its own accounts, and its tax codes (standard, zero rated, exempt …), each with its rate from a date. Items name their tax code in Item Master; customers and vendors their registration type, state or emirate, and tax number in Customer Master and Vendor Master.

Customer Invoices, Vendor Invoices and their returns take their tax from here, line by line. Retail Sales and the tax reports follow in later releases: until then they work as before, with one tax % per document.

Before you start

  • The tax accounts exist in the Chart of Accounts: for UAE VAT an output and an input VAT account; for India GST one of each for CGST, SGST (or UTGST), IGST and, if used, cess.
  • You need Tax Configuration's setup right to change it (Admin by default); access lets you look.

Step by step

  1. Open Tax Configuration and choose the company.
  2. With nothing set up yet, start from UAE VAT, India GST or **an own regime**. The preset fills in its components and tax codes; nothing is saved until you save.
  3. Choose the company's emirate (UAE VAT) or its GST state, the first two digits of its GSTIN (India GST).
  4. Rounding: per rate on the whole document (UAE VAT's default) or per line (India GST's).
  5. For each component, choose its output tax account (sales) and input tax account (purchases).
  6. Check the tax codes: their name, what kind each is, the recoverable % of input tax (100 unless part of it can't be reclaimed, the rest then being part of the cost), and Active.
  7. Save.

When a rate changes

Add a rate to the code with the date it applies from (Add a rate). A document takes the rate valid on its own date, so documents before the change keep the old rate. Don't change the old row.

India GST

Within the company's state a rate is charged as CGST and SGST (in a union territory such as Chandigarh, UTGST), half each; to another state, an SEZ or abroad, as IGST. Whether a sale is within the state comes from the customer's state (the place of supply). A code can carry a cess % as well. The preset holds the rates as revised from 22/09/2025 (5%, 18% and 40%, with 0.25% and 3% for stones and gold). The earlier 12% and 28% are kept, inactive, for documents dated before. Check the codes against your own items, and adjust them as needed.

A tax of your own

For another country's tax, start from an own regime: name up to six components (for example STATE and CITY), then give each code's rate as a % per component (6% STATE and 1.5% CITY make 7.5%).

Rights

access shows the configuration; setup changes it. A tax code that documents have used can't be removed (make it inactive), and the regime of a company whose documents use its codes can't change.

Questions and mistakes

  • "Choose the company's GST state": India GST needs it, to tell CGST + SGST from IGST.
  • "… belongs to another company": a component's account must be the company's own or shared by all companies.
  • "Tax code … is used by documents": untick Active instead.
  • For example: Example Trading LLC (Dubai) starts from UAE VAT, picks Dubai, Output VAT 2200 and Input VAT 1400, and saves; its SR code charges 5% from 01/01/2018.
