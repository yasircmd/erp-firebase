Opening Balances

Bring a company's position on its go-live date into the ERP: the trial balance, opening stock and unpaid invoices.

Screen: Opening Balances

What it is for

When a company starts using the ERP, its balances from the old system are brought in once, dated the day before go-live (go-live 1 January → opening date 31 December). Three kinds, each on its own tab:

  • GL: the trial balance (every account except the control accounts);
  • Stock: quantity and unit cost per item and warehouse;
  • Open Invoices: each vendor's and customer's unpaid documents and advances, so statements, aging and payment allocation work from day one.

Before you start

  • Opening Balances is for Admin users.
  • An Equity account for Opening Balance Clearing (for example "Opening Balance Clearing").
  • Items, warehouses, vendors and customers already set up (Master Data Import helps).

Step by step

  1. Choose the Company, set the Opening date and the **Opening Balance Clearing account, and select Save setup**.
  2. On a tab, select Copy headers (or **Copy headers with a sample row**), paste into Excel, fill it in, then copy the rows and paste them into the grid.
  3. Select Check. Every problem is listed by row and column (an unknown account, a missing batch number, a document entered twice…). Fix them in Excel and paste again.
  4. When the check is clean, select Post. All rows post together, or none.
  5. Repeat for the other tabs. When all three are in, the **Clearing balance shown must be zero**: then the GL, stock and open invoices agree.

What each tab takes

TabColumns (required ones marked on the screen)
GLAccount, Debit, Credit, Description, Cost Center, Profit Center
StockItem, Warehouse, Quantity, Unit Cost, Batch No, Expiry Date
Open InvoicesParty Type, Party, Document Type (Invoice / Advance), Document No, Document Date, Due Date, Amount, Description

What happens in the books

All dated on the opening date:

  • GL: one entry with your rows; the difference goes to Clearing.
  • Stock: the stock comes in at your cost (Dr each item's Inventory account, Cr Clearing).
  • Open Invoices: one entry per party on its control account, each document kept with its own number, date and due date, so payments can be allocated against it like any invoice.

Inventory, Trade Payables and Trade Receivables are not entered on the GL tab: their balances come from the Stock and Open Invoices tabs.

Corrections

A posted batch can be Reversed as a whole (Admin, any time), then a corrected one posted. A reversal is refused when a document in it has already been paid, or the stock has since been used; undo those first.

Good to know

  • Amounts are in the default currency; quantities in the item's base unit.
  • For a vendor or customer in a foreign currency, the Amount is in its currency and Rate (optional) gives the rate; blank, the rate on the document date from Currency Configuration. The entry is in the default currency at that rate.
  • Dates in the paste may be dd/mm/yyyy or yyyy-mm-dd; at most 2,000 rows.
  • The opening date and clearing account are fixed while any batch is posted.
