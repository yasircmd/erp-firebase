Customer and Vendor Aging

How old each party's open balance is, in buckets, with advances and pending cheques shown apart.

Screens: Customer Aging, Vendor Aging

What it is for

Shows, for every customer (or vendor) with something open, how much is not yet due and how much is overdue by 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 or more than 90 days — to chase collections or plan payments.

Step by step

  1. Open Customer Aging or Vendor Aging.
  2. Choose the Company and the As at date (today by default).
  3. Choose Age by:
    • Days overdue (from the due date): Not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90;
    • Days since the invoice date: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120, over 120.
  4. Select Run.
  5. Each row is one party: payment terms, the buckets, Owed, Unapplied credits (advances and credits not matched to an invoice), Net balance, and Pending PDCs (shown, not deducted).
  6. Select a row to see its open documents; SOA opens the party's statement.
  7. Copy to Excel or Print.

Good to know

  • The report checks its total against the parties' accounts in the General Ledger at that date and says whether they agree.
  • Each party's figures agree with its statement of account at the same date.
