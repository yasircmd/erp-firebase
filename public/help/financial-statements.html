Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

The three financial statements, with drill-down from each figure to the ledger.

Screens: Profit and Loss Statement (P&L), Balance Sheet (BS), Cash Flow Statement (CFS)

What it is for

StatementShowsPeriod
Profit and Loss (P&L)Revenue, cost of sales and expenses, and the profitFrom – To
Balance SheetAssets, liabilities and equityAs of a date
Cash Flow StatementCash in and out, by Operating, Investing and FinancingFrom – To

Step by step

  1. Open the statement and choose the Company.
  2. Choose the period (From and To) or, for the Balance Sheet, the As Of date.
  3. Narrow by Division, Branch, Cost Center or Profit Center if needed (for example a P&L per cost center).
  4. Select Run Report.
  5. Select an account line to open its General Ledger; Back returns.
  6. Copy Table to Excel, or Print.

Good to know

  • The P&L leaves out the year-end closing entry, so a closed year still shows its profit.
  • The Cash Flow is by the direct method: each cash or bank movement is classed by the account on its other side. That account's **Cash Flow Category** comes from its header in the Chart of Accounts; without one it counts as Operating.
  • The figures follow the posted entries only; drafts are not included.
