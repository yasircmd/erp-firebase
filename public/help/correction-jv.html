Correction JV (CJV)

Move a posted entry's lines to the right account, cost centre, profit centre or project, by a new CJV entry.

Screen: Correction JV (CJV)

What it is for

An entry was posted with the wrong coding: an expense on the wrong account, a cost on the wrong cost centre, a revenue line without its project. A Correction JV fixes it without touching the entry. It posts a new entry, numbered CJV-..., that takes each chosen line's old coding out and puts the right coding in, for the same amount.

Only the account, cost centre, profit centre and project change. The amount, the date of the original, its party and its description stay as they are.

Before you start

  • You need Correction JV's draft right to make one and its post right to post it (Roles and Permissions).
  • The entry must be posted and not reversed. Any posted entry can be corrected: a JV, a Cash & Bank voucher, an invoice's or a delivery's entry, a payroll journal.
  • If Approval Configuration has levels for Correction JV (CJV), a CJV waits for them before it posts.

Step by step

  1. Open Correction JV and choose New Correction.
  2. Choose the Company, type the entry's voucher number (as on the GL Report, e.g. JV-01-2026-000012) and choose Find. Its lines appear.
  3. Tick each line to correct. Its coding is copied below it: change the account, cost centre, profit centre or project.
  4. To spread a line over several codings, choose Split and give each part its amount (for example 60.00 to one cost centre, 40.00 to another). The parts must add up to the line: Parts 100.00 of 100.00.
  5. The Date is the original's by default. Change it if that month is closed: the correction must be in an open month, and not after this month.
  6. Save keeps it as a draft; Post posts it (or sends it for approval).

Each corrected line gives two lines on the CJV: the old coding on the opposite side, which takes it out, and the new coding (or each part) on the line's own side. The CJV always balances. From its date, the Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, the GL Report, Budget vs Actual and the Project Report show the new coding.

Lines that are not corrected

A lock beside a line says why it can't be ticked:

  • A customer's, vendor's or sales rep's line. Its account is the party's control account, which must always agree with the party's statement, and its party is part of the allocations. A line posted to the wrong party is fixed with a Journal Entry: credit the wrong party and debit the right one.
  • A line on an asset's account kept by Fixed Assets: use the Fixed Asset screens.
  • A line already corrected by another CJV: correct that CJV instead, or reverse it first.

On a line on an inventory account or a tax account, only the cost centre, profit centre and project can change: the stock ledger and the tax reports must still agree with those accounts. Nothing can be moved to an inventory, tax, control or asset account either.

Reversing

Reverse on a posted CJV puts the old coding back (a reversal CJV on the date you give).

An entry with a correction can't be reversed, by its own screen or as a JV, until the correction is reversed: otherwise the old coding would be taken out twice. The message names the CJV: reverse it first, then the entry.

Printing

Print on a posted CJV prints its lines with their coding, the entry it corrects and its totals.

Common questions

  • Is the original entry changed? No. It stays as posted. The CJV is a separate entry that names the original it corrects.
  • Can I correct the amount? No. Reverse the entry and post it again with the right amount.
  • The new account refuses my cost centre. The account's rules in the Chart of Accounts decide which cost centres, profit centres and projects it takes, as on any entry.
