Statements of Account (Vendor and Customer)

Everything posted with one vendor or customer over a period, the running balance, what is still open, and pending cheques.

Screens: Vendor Statement of Account (SOA), Customer Statement of Account (SOA)

What it is for

A statement of account (SOA) shows one vendor's or customer's history for a period: opening balance, every invoice, payment, receipt, note and adjustment with a running balance, the closing balance, and what is still open by document.

Step by step

  1. Open Vendor Statement of Account or **Customer Statement of Account**.
  2. Choose the Company and the vendor or customer.
  3. Choose From and To, and select Run.
  4. Read it:
    • the rows with debit, credit and balance;
    • open items: each unpaid document with its due date, days overdue and outstanding amount, and the aging of what is open;
    • pending PDCs: cheques not yet cleared at the end date;
    • a check against the party's account in the General Ledger.
  5. Select a document number to open it (Back returns to the statement).
  6. Print for the party, or CSV to open it in Excel.

Good to know

  • The open items always add up to the closing balance, because every amount on a party's account is allocated (see Paying and allocating).
  • The print leaves out the internal GL check.
