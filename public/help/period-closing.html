Period Closing (month-end and year-end)

Check and close each month so nothing more can be posted into it, and close the financial year into Retained Earnings.

Screen: Period Closing

What it is for

Closing a month locks it: nothing can be posted, reversed or approved with a date in it, so reported figures stay as reported. Closing the year moves its profit or loss into Retained Earnings.

Before you start

On the Setup tab, per company:

  • the Financial year: January – December or April – March (fixed after the first year-end);
  • the Retained Earnings account;
  • the checklist: each item set to Block (the month can't close while it finds something), Warn (listed, but the month can still close) or Off (not checked).
Checklist item
Drafts dated in the period, not posted
Documents waiting for approval
Recurring journals due and not posted
Depreciation not run for the month
Payroll for the month not posted
Post-dated cheques matured and not cleared
Deliveries posted, not invoiced
Customer Aging agrees with the receivables accounts
Vendor Aging agrees with the payables accounts
Stock value agrees with the inventory accounts
The Trial Balance balances

Two lists of your own, also each Block, Warn or Off:

  • Accounts that must be zero at month end: clearing and suspense accounts, such as a GRN clearing or payroll clearing account. A balance on the account at the month's end is a finding, with the amount.
  • Steps to tick before closing: your own steps, such as "Bank reconciliation done" or "Petty cash counted". Until the step is ticked for the month, it is a finding.

Step by step

Close a month

  1. Open Period Closing, choose the Company; the Months grid shows the year.
  2. On the first open month, run the Checklist: each item shows what it found, with the documents.
  3. Tick the steps you have done. Anyone who can close months can tick them; the Log keeps who ticked and when, and the closed month keeps its ticked steps. If the month is reopened, its steps are ticked again before it closes again.
  4. Fix what blocks, then select Close. Months close in order.

Reopen a month

Select Reopen on it and give the reason. The months after it reopen too; the ones before stay closed. Every close and reopen is in the Log.

Close the year

When all twelve months are closed (and the previous year is closed), select Close the year. After you confirm, a Year-End Closing entry (YEC-…) dated on the last day of the year moves every income, cost of sales and expense balance, by account and cost center, to Retained Earnings. Reopen the year (with a reason) reverses that entry; a month of a closed year can't be reopened until its year is.

What happens in the books

Closing a month posts nothing. Closing the year posts the Year-End Closing entry. The P&L leaves it out; the Trial Balance can show the year before it.

Payroll and depreciation in a closed month

Reversing or redoing a payroll run, a settlement, a loan or depreciation of a closed month offers to date it in the first open month instead; the entry records the original date.

Rights

Viewing and the checklist: Period Closing access. Closing and reopening are separate rights (by default Admin and Finance Manager).

Questions and mistakes

  • "yyyy-mm is closed for …" on any screen: the document's date is in a closed month. Use an open month's date, or reopen the month here.
  • Can't close: a blocking checklist item found something; open it to see the documents.
