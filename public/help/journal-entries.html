Journal Entries (JV)

Record any accounting entry by debits and credits: accruals, corrections, reclassifications, write-offs.

Screen: Journal Entry Processing (JV)

What it is for

A Journal Voucher (JV) records an entry directly in the accounts: debit and credit lines that must balance. Use it for anything no other screen records: accruals and provisions, corrections, reclassifications, discounts and write-offs, settling an advance against a bill.

For money in or out of a cash or bank account, use Cash and Bank Transactions instead.

Before you start

  • The accounts exist and are postable (see Chart of Accounts).
  • Expense, revenue and cost-of-sales lines need a cost center.

Step by step

  1. Open Journal Entry Processing (JV) and select New Journal Entry.
  2. Fill the header: Company, Entry Date, Description, and if used the Reference, Division, Branch, Cost Center and Profit Center (the header's cost and profit centers are the lines' defaults).
  3. Add the lines: Account Code, Debit or Credit, description, and the cost or profit center where needed. You can paste lines from Excel into the grid.
  4. A line on a vendor's or customer's account needs the **Vendor / Customer, and its Allocate** (see Paying and allocating).
  5. Check that total debits equal total credits.
  6. Save as Draft to finish later, or Post Entry. Where an approval is set up, the entry goes for approval instead (see Approvals).

Correcting a posted entry

A posted entry can't be edited. Select Reverse Entry, choose the reversal date (in an open month): a mirror entry is posted and the original stays on record. Then enter the right one. The description of a posted entry can still be corrected with the edit button next to it.

Entries posted by other screens (invoices, cheques, payroll, opening balances…) are reversed from their own screen; the JV screen refuses them and says where.

What happens in the books

When posted, each line is recorded on its account and date, and the entry gets its numbers: the JV number (per company and year) and the Txn Ref (the finance reference, shared with other finance documents). It shows in the General Ledger, Trial Balance and the statements.

Rights

Viewing, drafting and posting are separate rights (Roles & Permissions). Approval, where set up, is by the approvers in Approval Configuration.

Questions and mistakes

  • "…is closed for …": the entry date is in a closed month. Use a date in an open month, or ask for the month to be reopened (Period Closing).
  • A cost center is required: expense, revenue and cost-of-sales lines need one.
  • A party is required: a line on a vendor's payables or customer's receivables account must name that party, and only that party's own account may be used.
