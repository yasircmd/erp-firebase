Built modules

Modules your provider built for your business (rooms, bookings, investments, subscriptions …): their lists, records, documents, statuses and approvals.

What they are

Some modules in your menu are built modules: your ERP provider made them for your kind of business from settings, for example Rooms and Bookings for a hotel, Investments for an investment company, Properties and Leases for real estate. They work like the rest of the ERP and all look alike, so this page covers every one of them.

There are two kinds:

  • Master data: a list of records, each with a Code and a Name and the module's own fields (for example a room's type and rate).
  • Documents: numbered documents (for example BK-26-000001), each with a Date, its fields, often a table of lines with totals, and a status (for example Draft, Confirmed, Checked out).

A built module never posts to the accounts by itself: when it has actions, it makes a Customer Invoice, Vendor Invoice or Journal Entry that the accounts take as usual (see To the books).

Step by step

  1. Open the module from the menu. The list shows its records, newest first for documents. Use Find to search, Status to filter, Copy to paste the list into Excel.
  2. New opens an empty record. Choose the company first when it asks: the choices of a linked field (a customer, an item, an employee, a room …) are that company's.
  3. Fill in the fields. A field marked \* is required. A link field is a list to choose from (a customer, an item, a project, a room …); something switched off or a closed project is no longer offered. A calculated field (greyed, for example Nights or Amount) is worked out as you type and again when you save: see Calculated fields.
  4. On a document, add its lines with Add line; number and amount columns are totalled at the bottom.
  5. Save. A new document gets its number when first saved.
  6. Move a document on with the → status buttons. A status may need approval first: the document then waits, shows Waiting for approval, and the approvers see it in their Home tasks (with Approve and Reject on the document). A rejection keeps the document where it was, with the reason, until it is corrected and sent again.
  7. Delete removes a document while it is still in its first status (its number is not used again). A master record is switched off, never deleted, since documents may name it.
  8. Print prints the record or the document with its lines.

Board and calendar

Some built modules have tabs above their list:

  • Board (on master data, for example Rooms): one column per state, each record as a card in its state on the day chosen (today at first; use the arrows or the date). A record's state comes from the documents that book it that day (for example Booked or Occupied), else it is Free. A state set by hand (for example Cleaning or Out of order) wins: choose it on the card, or none to clear it. A card's document opens from it.
  • Calendar (on a document, for example Bookings): the records booked (rooms, vehicles, units) as rows and 14 days as columns, each document a bar from its start to its end in its state's colour. The arrows move a week; Today comes back. Select a bar to open its document. Only documents in a status that holds the record show (not a draft or a cancelled one).

No double booking. A document can't hold a record another document already holds for overlapping days: saving or moving it to such a status is refused, naming the other document, for example 101 · Garden room is already held by BK-26-000004 from 20/11/2026 to 22/11/2026. Whether the end date is the day the record is free again (a hotel: a guest may arrive on another's departure day) or its last day in use (a rental) is set for each module.

Calculated fields

Some fields are worked out for you, from the record's other fields:

  • a × b, for example a line's Amount = Qty × Rate, or a booking's Room charges = Nights × Rate;
  • a sum or a − b, for example Net due = This claim − Retention;
  • a percentage, for example Retention = 10 % of This claim;
  • days between two dates, for example Nights from Arrival to Departure;
  • earlier documents' total, for example a progress claim's Claimed before: the same field added up over the earlier documents of the same contract (by date, then by when they were made), a cancelled one not counted. On a line it adds up the earlier documents' lines of the same item, so a line's This claim is its value to date less what was claimed before.

You can't type in them; amounts are rounded to 2 places. They are kept with the record when it is saved, and add up into the totals and the reports like any amount. An earlier document changed later doesn't change the documents after it until they are saved again.

To the books

A built module may have actions that make a document in the accounts from a record, for example Invoice rent on a lease, Bill the guest on a room-service order, or Agent commission as a vendor invoice. Each action makes one Customer Invoice, Vendor Invoice or Journal Entry:

  1. Open the record. In the To the books panel, its action buttons show when the record may use them (for a document, in the statuses the action is for).
  2. Select the action and confirm. The document is made through its own screen's checks (your rights on it, an active customer or vendor, postable accounts, the sales type, tax, the period), as a draft to check and post on its screen, or submitted: posted, or sent to its approvers where Approval Configuration sets them. Item lines on a customer invoice deliver the stock, as the invoice's own item lines do (the company must allow invoices to deliver stock).
  3. The panel lists what was made; select a number to open it. The document's Linked documents show the record it came from.

An action may make its invoice as a variant, for example a booking's check-out makes a Room Invoice, with its own numbers and the room and nights in its extra fields. It may take the project from the record (a contract's project), else from the module's Setup.

An action makes its document once. Doing it again is refused while that document is still there and not reversed; delete the draft or reverse the posted document first.

Runs (subscriptions, rent, fees). An action may be a run: each record has a next-due date and a period (Monthly, Quarterly, Half-yearly or Yearly). On the Runs tab, choose the run and the date, then Propose: every record due on or before that date is listed, ticked. Untick any, then Run the ticked. One document is made per customer (a line for each of their records) or per record, as the module is set. Each record's next-due date then moves on by its period, so nothing is billed twice. A record that is refused (for example an inactive customer) stays due and doesn't stop the others. Past runs lists who ran what, what was made and what was refused, with the reason. A record behind by several periods is billed one period a run; run again for the next.

Setup (the tab). The accounts, sales type, cost centre, profit centre, project and warehouse an action uses when the record itself doesn't give them, set for each company. Changing it needs the right to change the module and to post the documents it makes.

Variants

A variant is one of the ERP's own documents under another name, made for your business: for example a Room Invoice (a Customer Invoice), a Utility Bill (a Vendor Invoice), an Event Order (a Sales Order), a Tour Quote (a Quotation) or an Accrual (a Journal Entry). It has its own menu entry and opens the original's own screen, with:

  • its own name on the screen and on the print (a tax invoice keeps its own heading, with the variant's name under it);
  • some of the original's fields renamed (for example Guest for Customer), a few hidden, and defaults on a new document;
  • its own extra fields in a panel under the header (for example Room and Nights), kept with each document and printed under the header;
  • its own numbers (for example RINV-26-01-000001), its own rights in Roles & Permissions and its own row in Approval Configuration.

Everything else is the original's: the same checks, posting and reports. A Room Invoice is a customer invoice in the books, the customer's statement, ageing and the tax returns. Someone with only the variant's rights sees and opens only the variant's documents; someone with the original's rights sees all of them on the original's screen, each marked with its variant's name.

Packs

Your provider may give you a pack: a set of built modules for your business, for example Hotel or Construction, switched on together with a menu folder, starter reports and a setup checklist. See Packs.

In reports

Each built module is a data set in Build a report (MIS Reports): a master's records, a document's documents by month and, for a document with lines, its lines. A link to another built master brings that master's fields, for example bookings by room type. A variant's documents are in Sales or Purchase lines, with a Variant column and the variant's extra fields. Saved reports from them can go on the MIS Dashboard.

Rights

Each built module has its own rights in Roles & Permissions: access to open it, write to add and change records and move documents on, and for documents void to move one to a final status (for example Cancelled). Approvals are set in Approval Configuration, under the module's name.

Questions and mistakes

  • A status button is missing: a final status needs the void right; a document waiting for approval has none until it is decided.
  • Save is missing: the document's status no longer allows changes (only the statuses set so allow them, the first one always).
  • A choice is missing from a list: it belongs to another company, or it was switched off.
  • An action button is missing: the record is not in a status the action is for, it is waiting for approval, it was already done (once), or you don't have the right to create (or, for a submitted one, post) that kind of document.
  • "no account … set it in the module's Setup": the record gives no account and the company's Setup has none for that action: set it on the Setup tab.
