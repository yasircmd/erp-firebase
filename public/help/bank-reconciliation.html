Bank Reconciliation

Match a bank account's entries in the ERP with the bank's statement, month by month, and see what is still to clear.

Screen: Bank Reconciliation

What it is for

A bank reconciliation shows that the bank account in the ERP agrees with the bank's statement for a month. Most differences are timing: cheques paid but not yet presented, deposits made but not yet credited. Those are listed, and once they are accounted for the difference must be nothing.

Step by step

  1. Choose the Company, the Bank account (an account marked Bank in the Chart of Accounts) and the Month, then select Open.
  2. Type the statement's Opening and Closing balance and select Save the balances. The last reconciled month's closing balance is shown for reference.
  3. Paste the statement from Excel (optional): copy the month's rows (date, description, reference, withdrawal, deposit, or one amount, negative for a withdrawal) and select Add the rows. Rows that can't be read (no date, or no amount) are listed.
  4. Accept the suggestions: lines with the same amount within a week are paired. A reference or cheque number found in the statement's text comes first.
  5. Match the rest by hand. Tick the ERP's lines and the statement's lines that go together, then select Match the lines selected. The totals shown must agree. Several ERP lines can match one bank line, and the other way round. An entry and its reversal can be matched together without any bank line.
  6. Without a pasted statement, tick the ERP's lines the bank shows and select Mark the GL lines selected cleared.
  7. A statement line with nothing in the ERP (bank charges, interest, a direct debit): select Post entry beside it and type the account it goes to (and a cost centre code after a space if the account needs one). A Bank Payment or Bank Receipt is posted for it, dated as the statement line, as Cash & Bank would post it (with its rights, numbering and approval), and is matched at once. If the entry needs approval first, match it once it is posted.
  8. Watch the Reconciliation panel until the Difference is 0.00.
  9. Submit. It goes to the approvers set in Approval Configuration for Bank Reconciliation (with none set, it is approved at once). Once approved, the month is locked.

To undo a match or a cleared mark, select it (Match 2 ✕, Cleared ✕). Only what is not yet cleared hides what is done.

Example: Example Trading LLC's bank shows 2,750.00 at the month's end. The ERP shows 2,050.00, and a cheque of 700.00 written on the 30th is not yet presented, so the balance per the bank is 2,750.00 and the difference is nothing.

Locking and reopening

  • A month can be submitted only when its difference is nothing and the account's previous month is approved: the months go in order.
  • A locked month can't be changed. Someone with the reopen right can Reopen it, giving a reason (kept in its history). Only the latest locked month of an account can be reopened.
  • A rejected month shows the reason; change it and submit again.
  • Period Closing checks that each bank account with entries has its month approved (Period Closing).

From month to month

A line cleared in a month doesn't appear again in later months. What is still not cleared carries into the next month. A bank account in a foreign currency is reconciled in its own currency.

Rights

In Roles & Permissions, Bank Reconciliation: access reads; prepare types the balances, pastes the statement, matches and marks lines cleared, posts an entry for a statement line (which also needs Cash & Bank's rights), submits and deletes a draft; reopen reopens a locked month. Approving follows the approval levels.
