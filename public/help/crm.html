CRM

Leads, opportunities through their stages, and the activities that follow them up, with Convert to Customer.

Screen: CRM

What it is for

CRM keeps track of the people and businesses you are trying to sell to, before and after they become customers:

  • a lead is someone who is not a customer yet;
  • an opportunity is a possible sale, to a lead or a customer, moving through stages until it is Won or Lost;
  • an activity is a call, meeting, email, visit or task with a due date.

Choose the company at the top. The screen has the tabs Leads, Opportunities, Activities and, for a CRM Manager, Setup.

Step by step

  1. A CRM Manager checks Setup once: the stages, lost reasons, lead sources and activity types.
  2. In Leads, add the lead with New Lead.
  3. From the lead, add an opportunity (New Opportunity) and the next call or visit (New Activity).
  4. Move the opportunity along the stages on the Board as it advances.
  5. When it buys: Won on the opportunity, and Convert to Customer on the lead. When it does not: Lost, with the reason.

Who sees what

Each lead, opportunity and activity has an owner. You see the ones you own. A CRM Manager (a right given in Roles) sees every record of their companies, can give a record to someone else (change its Owner) and changes Setup. Sales reps can own records too: they keep their own leads, opportunities and activities from their phone (see Sales reps in the Employee Portal). A rep's records are the rep's; the CRM Manager sees and reassigns them here.

Leads

  1. Select New Lead, and fill in the organisation or the contact's name, and a phone or an email.
  2. Choose where the lead came from (Source) and select Save.

If the phone, email or name matches another lead or an existing customer, the screen says which, so you do not enter the same business twice. You can still save it.

From a lead:

  • New Opportunity, New Activity and New Quotation start one for this lead.
  • Disqualify closes a lead that will not buy; say why.
  • Convert to Customer makes it a customer when it buys. The customer form is filled from the lead; choose the Classification and the Trade Receivables account (as in Customer Master, with the same rules) and select Save customer and convert. The lead's open opportunities, activities and quotations move to the new customer.

Opportunities

An opportunity is for a lead or a customer, with its Expected value and Expected close date. It starts at the first stage.

  • Board shows a column per stage with the number of opportunities and their value. Drag a card to another column to move it to that stage.
  • List shows them as a table.
  • The line above shows the total and the weighted value (each value times its stage's win probability).
  • New Quotation makes a quotation for it.
  • Won closes it as won. Lost asks why, from the list of reasons. A won or lost opportunity cannot be changed.

Activities

An activity is about a lead, an opportunity or a customer, with a type, a subject and a due date (and a time if you like).

  • Activities due today, or overdue, appear in your Home tasks; overdue ones in amber. Select one to open it.
  • Done asks what came of it, and offers to schedule the next one (a week later, by default).
  • Cancel Activity closes one that will not happen.

Setup

A CRM Manager sets, for each company:

  • the stages, in order, each with its win probability (Won and Lost always follow);
  • the lost reasons, lead sources and activity types;
  • how many days a quotation stays valid (see Quotations).

Until Setup is saved, the defaults apply: New 10 %, Qualified 25 %, Proposal 50 %, Negotiation 75 %.

Common messages

  • "That lead is not yours." Someone else owns it; ask a CRM Manager.
  • "Only a CRM Manager can give a record to someone else." You can only own new records yourself.
  • "Choose why it was lost." Pick a reason from the list.
