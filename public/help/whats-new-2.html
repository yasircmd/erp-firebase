What's new (page 2)

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

← Newer changes · Page 2 of 5 · Older changes →

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

06/11/2026

  • CRM: leads, opportunities and activities. A new CRM screen keeps your leads (with a warning when one looks like an existing lead or customer, and Convert to Customer), your opportunities on a stage board (drag a card to move it; Won, or Lost with the reason), and your calls, meetings and visits, with those due on your Home tasks. Owners see their own; a CRM Manager sees all and sets the stages.

04/11/2026

  • The screen's name once. An open screen showed its name twice, in the top bar and again as a large heading; now it shows once, in the top bar. See Working with screens.

03/11/2026

  • Cash and Bank: the customer or vendor on the print. A receipt or payment on a customer's or vendor's account prints the party's code and name on its line, and, when there is one party, at the top ("Received from" or "Paid to"). See Cash and Bank.
  • Cash and Bank: receipts print as Cash Receipt or Bank Receipt. A printed Cash Receipt or Bank Receipt is now titled by its type (it said "Payment Receipt"); payments keep their voucher title. See Cash and Bank.
  • Cash and Bank: only the chosen type is ticked. All five transaction types showed a tick; now only the one chosen does (the same for a Job Order Request's origin). See Cash and Bank.
  • Every entry screen: the compact layout. The last ones too: Recurring journals, the fixed asset entries (capitalization, capital work in progress, retirement, additional depreciation), Employee Changes, Loans, Interim payments, Final settlement and Budget: the title bar and its buttons stay at the top.
  • Purchase, cash and stock screens: the compact layout. Purchase Order, Goods Receipt Note, Purchase Return, Cash and Bank and Post-dated cheques, Stock Adjustment, Material Issue, Warehouse Movement, Physical Count, Production Entry and the Purchase and Inventory Purchase requisitions now keep the title bar, the buttons (Save, Post and the others, moved up from the bottom) and the key fields at the top while you scroll; the dimensions and the description share one row. A field's longer note shows when you point at it. See Working with screens.
  • Delivery Note, Customer Invoice and Sales Return: the new layout and Project. Delivery Note, Customer Invoice and Sales Return have the compact layout, with the title bar, buttons and key fields staying at the top. Each has a Project: a Delivery Note takes its Sales Order's project, an invoice its Delivery Notes' or order's (both changeable), and a return its invoice's. An invoice's direct lines can each have their own project. A reversal now posts even after its project is closed.
  • Project on entry screens, and narrower dates. On Vendor Invoice, Sales Order and Journal Entry, the Project field now comes after Cost Center and Profit Center, once the company has projects. It is the default for new lines; a Vendor Invoice's direct lines and a Journal Entry's lines can each have their own. Date fields take only the width a date needs, leaving more room for the vendor, customer and description. More screens follow.

02/11/2026

  • A more compact entry layout (first screens). On Vendor Invoice, Sales Order and Journal Entry, the header takes about half the space it did: the key fields in one row, the dimensions and description in another, adjustments beside the totals. The title bar with the buttons (Save, Post, Confirm...) and the key fields stay at the top while you scroll the lines. See Working with screens. The other entry screens follow.
  • Sales Order: delivered quantities, Delivered, and Close. An opened Sales Order now shows each line's Delivered and Remaining after a Delivery Note or a delivering invoice (it showed none delivered). An order whose lines are all delivered becomes Delivered on its own. Close Sales Order ends what is left of an order, with a reason: its reserved stock is released and no more deliveries or invoices are made from it; Reopen takes it up again.
  • Master Data Import: items fixed. On Master Data Import, an item's Tax Code and HSN / SAC are now saved, when adding items and when updating them (they were checked but not kept). Copy current records for items now gives the system's item code in the Item Code column, so the sheet can be pasted back, with your own code in User Code.
  • Chart of Accounts: Tree view. A new tab on the Chart of Accounts shows the chart as a tree, by account type and heading, like the Org chart on Employees. The filters apply, and an account's code opens its edit form.
  • GL Account Reconciliation. A new screen, GL Account Reconciliation, explains each month the balance of the accounts the company chooses (GRN Clearing, suspense, accruals, prepayments), by matching lines that offset each other or by a schedule of items. The months are approved and locked, and Period Closing checks them.

01/11/2026

  • Bank Reconciliation: post, approve and lock. A bank charge or interest on the statement is posted from its line and matched at once; a reconciled month is submitted, approved and locked, and reopened with a reason. Period Closing now checks that each bank account's month is approved (Bank Reconciliation).
  • Bank Reconciliation. A new screen, Bank Reconciliation, matches a bank account's entries with the bank's statement for a month. Paste the statement from Excel, accept the suggested matches, match the rest by hand, and see what is not yet presented or credited until the difference is nothing.
  • Home, by its name. The menu's title is now Home (with your company's name), and the button back to it from a screen is ← Home, in place of "ERP Module Hierarchy Tree" and "Back to Hierarchy" (Getting started).
  • Wider screens. Every module's screen now uses more of a large monitor (up to 1600px wide), with a margin kept at each side; Home keeps its column (Working with screens).
  • Customers and vendors typed in. On every screen, a customer or vendor is now chosen as an account is: type part of its code or name in the box and pick from the matches, however short the list (Working with screens).
  • The credit check. Confirming a Sales Order and posting a Customer Invoice now check the customer, where the company switches it on in Credit Control Setup: on credit hold, refused; over its limit or overdue, a warning or a block with an override, as the company chooses. The sales reps' portal shows each customer's credit left.

31/10/2026

  • Credit Control. A new screen, Credit Control, gives each customer a credit limit in each company, and a credit hold with its reason. It shows what each customer owes, what it has been delivered but not invoiced and its open orders, against its limit, with what is overdue: those on hold, over their limit or overdue first. The check on Sales Orders and Customer Invoices follows next.
  • Project budgets for a project's whole life. Where a company chooses it in Budget Setup, a project gets a budget of its own: one amount per account for its whole life, approved and revised like a year's. Its report shows each account's actual to date, what is left and the % used, and a Vendor Invoice of the project is checked against what is left.
  • Cash forecast. In Budget, the company's cash from today: the bank and cash now, then 13 weeks and the months to the year's end. From open invoices on their due dates, post-dated cheques, open orders, payroll and recurring entries, from the budget, or both (Setup). Overdue invoices are shown apart, not counted.
  • The budget's spending check. A Purchase Order on submit, and a Vendor Invoice's direct lines on post, are checked against the year's budget. Each company chooses, in Budget Setup: off, a warning (you may go ahead; the approvers see it) or a block (only someone who may override the budget goes ahead, with a reason); the period; and whether open orders count as spent.
  • Forecasts. In Budget, a forecast is the year you now expect for the P&L: the actuals so far, then your estimate of the months ahead, starting from the Current budget. Finalise one each month or quarter to see how the expectation moves; Budget vs Actual shows the year forecast beside the budget. Forecasts need no approval.

30/10/2026

  • Budget. The new Budget screen holds each company's budget for its financial year, by account and month, optionally per cost centre or project, for the P&L and the balance sheet. Start from last year's actuals or budget, paste from Excel, or spread a year total. Budgets are approved through Approval Configuration; a change later in the year is a revision, with the Original kept. Budget vs Actual compares it with the posted figures, month and year to date, better or worse coloured. Forecasts and the spending check follow.
  • Help on a phone. Every Help page has Back to the ERP, the way back from Help in the installed app. On a phone a page starts with its own text, not the list of pages; All pages jumps to the list. Bold text that ran over two lines in a list now shows as bold. In the app, Help always opens its latest version, even on a slow connection. See Help.
  • The ERP on a phone. Home, your tasks and the chosen reports (Trial Balance, aging, Stock Availability, the statements of account) now fit a phone's screen. A pending approval opens as a compact card where you approve, or reject with a reason. Other screens stay at a computer's width, with a notice you can close. See On a phone.
  • Install the ERP and the Employee Portal as apps. Each has its own icon and window and opens faster, because its pages and styles are kept on the device. Pages also load faster in the browser. See Installing the app.
  • Exchange rates from the sheet. In Currency Configuration, Get rates from the sheet reads the firm's Google Sheet of rates and shows each beside your last one. A move of more than 5% is marked. You tick the rates to use and apply them; none applies on its own.
