Tax Reports

A company's tax for a period from its posted documents: the tax summary, UAE VAT 201, and India's GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, ready to copy to Excel.

Screen: Tax Reports

What it is for

Tax Reports works out a company's tax for a period, ready for the person who files the return. It reads the documents posted with tax line by line (see Tax Configuration):

  • Customer Invoices and Sales Returns;
  • Retail Sales and their returns;
  • asset sales in Fixed Asset Retirement;
  • Vendor Invoices and Purchase Returns.

The reports are:

  • Tax summary, for any regime: sales and purchases by tax code and by component, with the part reclaimed, the part that went to cost, and reverse charge.
  • VAT 201, for UAE VAT: the boxes of the FTA's return.
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, for India GST: GSTR-1 as the sheets of the GST offline tool, and GSTR-3B's tables.

Filing stays outside: you enter the figures on the FTA or GST portal yourself.

Before you start

  • The company has a Tax Configuration.
  • Customers and vendors have their registration type, state or emirate, and tax number (GSTIN or TRN) in Customer Master and Vendor Master. The reports use what is there when you run them.
  • For India, items have their HSN or SAC code in Item Master, for GSTR-1's HSN summary.

Step by step

  1. Choose the Company. The Report list shows the reports its regime has.
  2. Choose the period: From and To, or This month, **Last month or Last quarter**.
  3. Click Run.
  4. Click a figure's row to see the documents behind it.
  5. Click Copy on a table and paste it into Excel. GSTR-1's sheets copy with the GST offline tool's headings, dates (for example 05-Oct-2026) and units, ready to paste into the tool's template.

How the figures are counted

  • Dates. A document counts on its own date. A Sales Return, Purchase Return or retail return counts minus.
  • Reversals. A reversed document stays in its own period. Its reversal counts minus on the reversal's date.
  • Currency. Amounts are in the company's base currency, at each document's exchange rate.
  • Documents with one tax %. A document posted before line tax, with one tax % for the whole document, is not counted. The screen says how many such documents fall in the period, with their tax and numbers, so you can add them from the old figures.
  • VAT 201:
    • boxes 1a–1g are standard-rated sales by emirate, the place of supply. A counter sale, or an asset sold to the bank, counts in the company's own emirate;
    • box 6 is goods from an overseas vendor under reverse charge, and box 3 is the rest of reverse charge;
    • box 9 is standard-rated purchases with the VAT you can reclaim;
    • box 14 is the tax due less the tax reclaimed;
    • adjustments (bad-debt relief, corrections, box 7) are made on the FTA portal.
  • GSTR-1:
    • B2B: registered buyers and SEZs;
    • B2CL: inter-state invoices to unregistered buyers above the **B2CL limit** in Tax Configuration (₹1,00,000 by default);
    • B2CS: the rest, by state and rate, Retail Sales included. A credit note to a consumer reduces it;
    • EXP: exports, with or without IGST;
    • CDNR and CDNUR: credit notes;
    • nil rated, exempt and non-GST supplies, the HSN summary (B2B and B2C apart), and the documents issued by series.
  • GSTR-3B:
    • 4(A) is the full input tax of each purchase, and 4(B) reverses the part the tax code doesn't let you reclaim;
    • 6.1 shows each component's tax payable, the net credit, and reverse charge, which is paid in cash. Setting off one tax's credit against another is done on the portal.

Rights

access runs the reports, for the companies you can open. Admin, Finance Manager and Accountant have it by default.

Questions and mistakes

  • "This company has no Tax Configuration": set its tax up first.
  • A figure looks wrong. Open its documents and check the party's registration type and state, and each line's tax code.
  • Documents are "not in this report": they were posted with one tax % before line tax.
  • For example: Example Trading LLC (Sharjah) runs VAT 201 for October. Box 1b shows Dubai's sales of 1,400.00 with VAT of 70.00, from invoices CI-1 and CI-2.
