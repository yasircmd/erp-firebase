General Ledger Report (Ledger and Listing)

Every line posted to an account, with its opening, running and closing balance; or a plain listing across several accounts.

Screen: General Ledger Reports (GL)

What it is for

The General Ledger shows what was posted to an account and why. It has two views:

  • Ledger: one account over a period, with opening balance, every line, running balance and closing balance;
  • Listing (several accounts): the lines of any accounts you pick, by date, without balances, with debit and credit totals.

Step by step

Ledger

  1. Open General Ledger Reports (GL), keep Ledger selected.
  2. Choose the Company, the Account, From and To.
  3. If needed, narrow by Division, Branch, Cost Center or Profit Center.
  4. Tick Include Draft entries to see drafts as well (marked DRAFT; their figures may still change).
  5. Select Run Report.

Listing

  1. Select Listing (several accounts).
  2. Pick an account and select Add (or press Enter); repeat for more. A header account brings in every account beneath it. Remove one with its ×.
  3. Choose the dates and filters, and run it.

From a line to its source

  • Select the Txn Ref to see the whole journal (GL Impact): every line with account, cost center, debit and credit. From there, Copy to Excel or Open source document (the invoice, payment, cheque… that posted it).
  • Select the Voucher No. to open the source document directly.

Copy Table and Print follow the view on screen.

Good to know

  • Reports such as the Trial Balance open the General Ledger for an account when you select it; Back returns.
  • A listing shows at most 20,000 lines; narrow the dates or accounts if asked.
