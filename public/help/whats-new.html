What's new

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

Page 1 of 5 · Older changes →

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

13/11/2026

  • The Audit Log. A new Admin screen shows who changed what, and when: every save, posting and approval, each changed field of a master or a setting (before and after), sign-ins and sign-outs, and refused attempts. Salaries, bank details and passwords show only that they changed. See Audit Log.
  • One sign-in page. Employees and sales reps with a portal account can sign in on the ERP's page with their Employee Code; anyone with no ERP access goes straight on to the Employee Portal. See Getting started.
  • Descriptions optional on stock entries. Left blank, a Goods Receipt, Material Issue, Stock Adjustment, Warehouse Movement, Physical Count, Production, Delivery Note or return saves a description of its own, and a blank line takes the item's name. See Working with screens.
  • A warehouse's default cost center. Set it in Organizational Units; a stock entry's line takes it when you choose the warehouse.
  • Short codes for every screen. Each screen now has a code of 2 to 4 letters beside it in the menu (COA, JV, GRN, PO and so on). Type the code in Search modules and press Enter to open it. See Getting started.
  • Enter and Esc on a screen. Enter on a list starts a new entry; Esc goes back to the report you came from, or Home, asking first if something isn't saved. See Working with screens.
  • Approvals at the foot of the entry. Your approval is needed now sits under the entry's lines on every screen, says your level and who decides next, and no longer goes missing when you open the document from a Home task. See Approvals.
  • The companies you choose apply everywhere. Home tasks, My dashboard, the MIS screens and Master Data Import now follow the companies chosen at the top of Home. See Choosing your companies.
  • Dashboard tile sizes. Tiles can be 1, 2 or 4 wide, and a chart can be tall, with two tiles stacked beside it. See MIS Dashboard.
  • Roles & Permissions. The row of role names stays at the top while you scroll the matrix. See Roles & Permissions.

12/11/2026

  • Packs for your kind of business. Your provider can now switch on a whole set of modules in one step: Hotel (rooms, bookings with no double booking, Room Invoices, table orders), Subscriptions, Real estate (units, leases, rent runs), Investments and Construction (contracts, bills of quantities, progress claims with retention and advance recovery, subcontracts, site reports). Each comes with a menu folder, starter reports and a setup checklist in Pack setup. See Packs.
  • Calculated fields in built modules. Amounts such as Nights × Rate, a retention percentage, the days between two dates or what earlier claims already claimed are now worked out for you. See Built modules.
  • Built modules in reports. In MIS Reports, Build a report now lists your built modules under their own heading: a master's records, a document's documents and its lines, with a linked master's fields (for example bookings by room type). Sales and Purchase lines have a Variant column and the variant's extra fields, so a Room Invoice's revenue can be shown on its own. See Build a report.
  • Variants of the ERP's documents. Your provider can now give you a document under your own name, for example a Room Invoice (a Customer Invoice), a Utility Bill, an Event Order, a Tour Quote or an Accrual. It opens the original's screen with its own name, labels, extra fields, numbers, rights and approvals, and its documents are the original's in every report. See Built modules.

09/11/2026

  • Built modules to the books. A built module can now make a Customer Invoice, Vendor Invoice or Journal Entry from a record (as a draft, or posted), and run billing in bulk, for example every subscription due this month, one invoice per customer, each next-due date moved on. See Built modules.
  • Boards and calendars for built modules. A built module can now show a Board (for example rooms by state today: Booked, Occupied, Cleaning, Free) and a Calendar (each booking a bar across the days), and refuses a double booking of the same room for overlapping days. See Built modules.
  • Built modules. Your ERP provider can now build modules for your kind of business (rooms and bookings, investments, properties and leases …) from settings. Each appears in your menu like any other module, with its own rights, numbering, statuses and approvals. See Built modules.
  • Task emails from your own mailbox. Each corporate now sends its task emails from its own mailbox, and each company can have its own: set the address and its app password in Company Master (Task emails mailbox, or the envelope on a company's row), then Send a test email. The password is saved encrypted and never shown again. With no mailbox set, task emails are not sent. See Company Master.

08/11/2026

  • Your tasks by email. The ERP can email you what waits for you: approvals within a few minutes, your rejected items, and a summary of your open tasks at the time and on the days you choose. Choose in Email settings (your name, top right), or turn the emails off. Employees are emailed their portal requests' and leave's updates, and can switch them off on the portal's Me page. See Getting started.
  • No zoom when you tap a box on a phone. Tapping Search modules (or any box on a screen laid out for a phone) no longer zooms the page in: the boxes use a slightly larger text on a phone. Pinch to zoom still works. See Getting started.
  • The MIS on a phone. MIS Reports and the MIS Dashboard now open at the phone's width: the report's lines as cards, the tiles one under another. Before, they opened at a computer's width.
  • Help for the MIS. A new Help area, Management reports (MIS): MIS Dashboard, MIS Reports (the comparisons, My reports, the MIS setup) and Build a report.
  • MIS Reports: Build a report. Choose Build a report… to make your own report from sales lines, purchase lines, GL lines or stock movements: over 1 to 24 months, with filters, grouped by one or two fields with totals (total, count, average, lowest, highest), as a pivot (rows × columns) or as a list; click a column to sort. Save it in My reports, share it, and show it on the MIS Dashboard as a chart or a top list. Each data set needs its own module's access too (for example Customer Invoice for sales lines).
  • MIS Reports: charts and My reports. Each report shows a chart above its table (bars, a line, or none); Copy takes the table for Excel and Print prints it; on a phone each line is a card. Save as… keeps a report with its company, comparison and chart, opening on the latest month or on the month you saved; finance managers can Share it with roles, whose users see it under "Shared with me".
  • MIS Dashboard. Under Financial Accounting, MIS Dashboard shows your tiles: key figures with their change, charts, top lists and gauges against a good band, worked out when you open it. Edit to add, remove and arrange tiles (each with its own comparison) and save your own; Reset goes back to your role's. Finance managers can make a dashboard the default for a role. Tick Show on Home to see your tiles on Home too (off unless you choose it).
  • A customer's sales rep is set in Customer Master. Customer Master has a Sales Rep field; the Sales reps tab in Sales Invoice Configuration now lists each rep's customers instead of choosing them. Quotations, Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, Customer Invoices and Retail Sales have a Sales Rep field too: the customer's rep (or the order's, along the chain) by default, changeable while a draft. A Sales Return takes its invoice's rep. See Customer Master and Sales Order.
  • MIS Reports: Sales by rep, each document's own rep, invoices and retail sales less their returns. And Expenses, Receivables, Payables and Sales by customer now show every line, not only the first.
  • MIS Reports, the first part. Under Financial Accounting, MIS Reports shows the key figures, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, ratios, bank and cash, expenses, receivables, payables, sales by customer, stock in hand and fast, slow and non-moving stock, each compared with the previous month, the same month last year, the year to date or the budget, with the change and its %. The dashboard, charts and your own saved reports follow.

07/11/2026

  • Postings when the system is busy. A posting now waits up to 20 seconds (it was 10) for another one to finish, then says "The system is busy posting; please try again in a moment." A long posting can no longer be overtaken by another while it is still running.
  • Correction JV (CJV). Move a posted entry's lines to the right account, cost centre, profit centre or project, a line split if needed, by a new CJV entry; the entry itself is not changed. Customers' and vendors' lines are not corrected (fix a wrong party with a JV). See Correction JV.
