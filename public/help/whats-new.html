What's new

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

Page 1 of 5 · Older changes →

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

08/11/2026

  • No zoom when you tap a box on a phone. Tapping Search modules (or any box on a screen laid out for a phone) no longer zooms the page in: the boxes use a slightly larger text on a phone. Pinch to zoom still works. See Getting started.
  • The MIS on a phone. MIS Reports and the MIS Dashboard now open at the phone's width: the report's lines as cards, the tiles one under another. Before, they opened at a computer's width.
  • Help for the MIS. A new Help area, Management reports (MIS): MIS Dashboard, MIS Reports (the comparisons, My reports, the MIS setup) and Build a report.
  • MIS Reports: Build a report. Choose Build a report… to make your own report from sales lines, purchase lines, GL lines or stock movements: over 1 to 24 months, with filters, grouped by one or two fields with totals (total, count, average, lowest, highest), as a pivot (rows × columns) or as a list; click a column to sort. Save it in My reports, share it, and show it on the MIS Dashboard as a chart or a top list. Each data set needs its own module's access too (for example Customer Invoice for sales lines).
  • MIS Reports: charts and My reports. Each report shows a chart above its table (bars, a line, or none); Copy takes the table for Excel and Print prints it; on a phone each line is a card. Save as… keeps a report with its company, comparison and chart, opening on the latest month or on the month you saved; finance managers can Share it with roles, whose users see it under "Shared with me".
  • MIS Dashboard. Under Financial Accounting, MIS Dashboard shows your tiles: key figures with their change, charts, top lists and gauges against a good band, worked out when you open it. Edit to add, remove and arrange tiles (each with its own comparison) and save your own; Reset goes back to your role's. Finance managers can make a dashboard the default for a role. Tick Show on Home to see your tiles on Home too (off unless you choose it).
  • A customer's sales rep is set in Customer Master. Customer Master has a Sales Rep field; the Sales reps tab in Sales Invoice Configuration now lists each rep's customers instead of choosing them. Quotations, Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, Customer Invoices and Retail Sales have a Sales Rep field too: the customer's rep (or the order's, along the chain) by default, changeable while a draft. A Sales Return takes its invoice's rep. See Customer Master and Sales Order.
  • MIS Reports: Sales by rep, each document's own rep, invoices and retail sales less their returns. And Expenses, Receivables, Payables and Sales by customer now show every line, not only the first.
  • MIS Reports, the first part. Under Financial Accounting, MIS Reports shows the key figures, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, ratios, bank and cash, expenses, receivables, payables, sales by customer, stock in hand and fast, slow and non-moving stock, each compared with the previous month, the same month last year, the year to date or the budget, with the change and its %. The dashboard, charts and your own saved reports follow.

07/11/2026

  • Postings when the system is busy. A posting now waits up to 20 seconds (it was 10) for another one to finish, then says "The system is busy posting; please try again in a moment." A long posting can no longer be overtaken by another while it is still running.
  • Correction JV (CJV). Move a posted entry's lines to the right account, cost centre, profit centre or project, a line split if needed, by a new CJV entry; the entry itself is not changed. Customers' and vendors' lines are not corrected (fix a wrong party with a JV). See Correction JV.
  • Sales Return on a closed project. A return whose invoice's project has since been closed is refused when you post it; reopen the project to post it. Reversing a posted return is still allowed. See Sales Return.

06/11/2026

  • Bank details on a bank account. In the Chart of Accounts, a bank account's Bank details keep its bank, account number, IBAN and SWIFT. Tick Print on invoices to print them on invoices, proformas and quotations, and Show in Cash & Bank and PDC to see them there and on their prints. See Chart of Accounts.
  • No entry after this month. A document can be dated at most the last day of the current month (on save and on post); payroll up to 12 months ahead. A post-dated cheque's date, delivery dates, Valid until and due dates stay free. See Working with screens.
  • Signed out after 30 minutes. The ERP now signs you out after 30 minutes without activity (it was 10), still warning 30 seconds before. See Getting started.
  • What's new in pages. This page shows the latest 30 changes; older ones are on the next pages (Older changes at the top and bottom).
  • Light theme: the yellows. Yellow and amber text (in Support, the e-invoice panel of Customer Invoice and elsewhere) is now a darker amber in the light theme, easy to read on white.
  • Item Sub-Category: Find. A Find box over the list looks up a sub-category by name, parent category or company. See Item Sub-Category.
  • Item Master: the sub-categories of a new item. A new item now lists its category's sub-categories at once, a company's too (named with the company; choosing one gives the item that company). See Item Master.
  • Roles & Permissions: unsaved ticks. Going back with permissions changed but not saved asks first. See Roles & Permissions.
  • Support: 10 new issues a day. A company can send at most 10 new issues a day; add to an open issue for more. See Support.
  • Light theme: Quotations and the CRM board. In the light theme, a quotation's line fields and the CRM stage board's columns showed dark; they now follow the theme. See Quotations and CRM.
  • CRM reports and lead import. CRM has a Reports tab: the pipeline by stage, owner and expected close month, win / loss with the win rate and the lost reasons, activities done and overdue, and lead sources with their conversions and won value, all in the base currency, each with Copy to Excel. See CRM. Leads can now be imported from Excel in Master Data Import: a row matching an existing lead or customer is shown and left out unless you tick Import matches too.
  • CRM on the sales rep's phone. In the Employee Portal's Sales tab, a rep now keeps their leads, pipeline and activities, and makes quotes at the price list's prices with a discount, submits them, shares the PDF and, when the customer accepts, sends the order to the office. See Sales reps in the Employee Portal.
  • Quotations. A new Quotations screen, in CRM, offers prices to a customer or a lead: the price list's prices, a discount and the tax, its own QT number, approval where Approval Configuration sets it, revisions (Rev 2, Rev 3) and a print to send. When the customer accepts, Create Sales Order makes the order from it. A CRM opportunity or lead has New Quotation.
  • CRM: leads, opportunities and activities. A new CRM screen keeps your leads (with a warning when one looks like an existing lead or customer, and Convert to Customer), your opportunities on a stage board (drag a card to move it; Won, or Lost with the reason), and your calls, meetings and visits, with those due on your Home tasks. Owners see their own; a CRM Manager sees all and sets the stages.

04/11/2026

  • The screen's name once. An open screen showed its name twice, in the top bar and again as a large heading; now it shows once, in the top bar. See Working with screens.

03/11/2026

  • Cash and Bank: the customer or vendor on the print. A receipt or payment on a customer's or vendor's account prints the party's code and name on its line, and, when there is one party, at the top ("Received from" or "Paid to"). See Cash and Bank.
  • Cash and Bank: receipts print as Cash Receipt or Bank Receipt. A printed Cash Receipt or Bank Receipt is now titled by its type (it said "Payment Receipt"); payments keep their voucher title. See Cash and Bank.
  • Cash and Bank: only the chosen type is ticked. All five transaction types showed a tick; now only the one chosen does (the same for a Job Order Request's origin). See Cash and Bank.
