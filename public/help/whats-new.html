What's new

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

16/10/2026

  • Chart of Accounts: classes, groups and renumbering. Codes now have a level each: a class per type (100000 Assets), groups under it (110000 Cash) and the accounts under a group (110001). An account can no longer sit straight under a class. Cash and bank accounts are the ones under a group marked Cash or Bank, not those with a certain code. Renumber gives a used account another code in its own group, and every entry and document follows. See Chart of Accounts.
  • Search any long list. Choosing an account, item, customer, vendor or a line's party now opens a searchable list. Type any part of the code or name ("trad" finds Beta Trading), choose with the arrow keys and Enter, and find your recent choices at the top. On a phone it fills the screen. See Working with screens.
  • End-of-service statement. A final settlement now prints an End-of-Service Settlement Statement for the employee (**Print statement**). It shows the service, the gratuity worked out by slab, entitlements, deductions and the net, in figures and in words. It is marked DRAFT until posted. Once the settlement is paid, the employee also finds it in the Employee Portal under My payslips. See Final Settlement.
  • Sell from stock on the invoice. Where a company switches it on (Sales Invoice Configuration → Invoices may deliver stock directly), a Customer Invoice can have item lines, typed or brought in **From Sales Order**. When it is posted, the invoice delivers them itself: a Delivery Note is made for them, the stock leaves at its cost and the order counts them as delivered. Print delivery slip prints the items without prices. See Customer Invoice.
  • Payroll: share an employee's cost across cost centers. A Transfer in Employee Change can now set a cost split by % (for example 60% / 40%). Every payroll expense of the employee is then shared that way. In a draft payroll run you can also change one employee's cost center (or split) for this run only, with a reason. The payslip now shows the cost center it posted to, even after a later transfer. See Employee Change and Payroll Run.
  • Paste lines from Excel into Purchase Requisition, Inventory Purchase Requisition and Sales Order: Copy Table gives the columns (also on an empty grid), Paste from Excel adds the rows. A row that isn't right stops the paste and is named. On a Sales Order the price list's price is used where the item has one. See Purchase Requisition, Inventory Purchase Requisition and Sales Order.
  • Fix a payment's matching without reversing it. A posted payment or receipt that settles the wrong bill: open it and select **Re-match references**, choose again, and give the reason. The books don't change, and it works in a closed month. It needs the new rematch right. See Paying and allocating.
  • Month-end checklist: a new check for **deliveries posted but not invoiced; your own accounts that must be zero** at month end (clearing accounts); and your own steps to tick before closing. See Period Closing.
  • Retail counter: find a customer by code, name or mobile number; an unknown mobile can become a new customer there. A mobile number now belongs to one customer of a company, also in Customer Master. See Retail Sales and Customer Master.
  • Shareholders in Company Master, with their % of the issued shares, their history and a printed company profile. They are seen with a new right, shareholders. See Company Master.
  • Tasks on Home: a posted delivery stays a task for the Invoicing team (or its maker) until it is invoiced; post-dated cheques coming due go to the Treasury team, 3 days before the cheque date (changeable). Both are set in Approval Configuration. See Approval Configuration.
  • Lists: choose how many rows a page holds. Journal entries, cash and bank, GRNs, purchase orders and the other document lists have Rows beside the count: 25, 50 or 100. Your choice is remembered in this browser for every list. See Working with screens.
  • Leaving reasons. Payroll Setup has a new Leaving Reasons tab (Resignation, Termination or Other), with common reasons to start and room for your own. An employee on notice or who has left must have one, with an optional note. See Payroll Setup and Employee Master.
  • A leaver's sign-in ends on its own. Someone leaving keeps the ERP and the Employee Portal until their final settlement is paid, and never more than 30 days after their last working day. HR can extend it, keep it, or go back to the rule, from the employee record. User Management shows "Leaving: access until …" or "Access ended". See Employee Master and User Management.
  • Employee Portal queries. An employee can now withdraw a payroll query (giving a reason) or mark it resolved, at any time while it is open. See Employee Requests.
  • Locations. Organizational Units has a new type, Location (an office, a site, a yard), shared by all companies or of one. Choose it on the employee record and on the fixed asset record; an asset's location can change at any time. See Organizational Units and Fixed Asset Master.
  • Item sub-categories by company. A sub-category can now be shared by all companies or belong to one. Item Master shows the shared ones and the item's company's, and New… beside Sub-Category opens Item Sub-Category to add one. See Item Sub-Category.
  • One print style. Printed documents (invoices, vouchers, orders, delivery notes and the like) now have a light, rounded frame and light lines between the cells. Payslips keep their own layout.

14/10/2026

  • User Management: two fixes. Adding a new user straight after editing another one no longer saves over the edited user. Last Login now shows when each user last signed in, in your local time. See User Management.
  • Retail Sales: the Items total is now the number of units on the sale; with a discount, the amount before it shows as Subtotal. See Retail Sales.
  • Chart of Accounts opens on Active accounts; choose All Statuses to see retired ones. See Chart of Accounts.
  • Approval Tracker can be filtered by status: Pending, Approved or Rejected. See Approval Tracker.
  • Inventory Aging: click an item code to open it in the Stock Ledger. See Inventory Aging.
  • Company Master lists companies in code order. See Company Master.
  • Help has a Light mode / Dark mode button, and the ERP's Help button no longer appears on prints. See Getting started.
