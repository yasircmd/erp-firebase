What's new

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

26/10/2026

  • Invoices taxed line by line. For a company with a Tax Configuration, each Customer Invoice line now takes its tax code from its item or Sales Type, at the rate on the invoice date: standard, zero-rated and exempt lines on one invoice, GST as CGST + SGST or IGST by the place of supply, and prices that include tax from a price list marked so. Each component posts to its own account and prints with its rate; Sales Returns take back their share. Changing a line's code needs the new **change tax code** right.
  • India's e-invoice, ready. Under India GST, each posted invoice and credit note to a registered business, an SEZ or a customer abroad gets its e-invoice as the IRP's JSON, checked for what it needs (GSTINs, addresses and PIN codes, HSN codes) and downloadable from the invoice. See Sales Invoice Configuration. Customers and vendors gain a City and PIN code.
  • Tax Configuration, for VAT, GST or a tax of your own. Each company now sets its sales tax in Tax Configuration: UAE VAT or India GST ready-made, or its own, with each component's accounts and its tax codes rated by date. Items take a tax code and HSN / SAC; customers and vendors a registration type, state or emirate, and a checked GSTIN or TRN. Invoices start using it in the next release.
  • Sales reps' receipts, final at once. A rep's collection in the Employee Portal now gives the customer its receipt straight away, numbered from the rep's own series (RC-AK-000001), shared as a PDF from the phone. The rep picks the invoices it pays, and sees the cash and cheques they hold. The cashier takes the cash with a Cash Receipt that chooses the rep; the office ticks the cheques received from the rep. See Sales reps, set up in Sales Invoice Configuration.

25/10/2026

  • Request types of your own. In Employee Requests HR sets up the requests employees raise from the portal: a salary certificate, IT support … each with its own form, straight to its handler or through the line manager first, an optional time to answer (overdue shown), and a document as the answer. Employees find them under Requests → Other; numbered RQ-.
  • Leaver clearance. The new Clearances screen takes each leaver through the company's steps (Finance, IT, HR by default), each signed off by its team, with a checklist and any amount to recover. The final settlement goes for approval once every step is signed; the leaver follows it in the Employee Portal.
  • Hold a salary. Payroll's new Salary holds tab stops an employee's salary from a month: the payslip shows a Stop Salary deduction and the amount waits on a Salary held account (set it in Payroll Setup). Released, it is paid with the next run. See Payroll.
  • Several post-dated cheques in one entry. Enter a customer's or vendor's set of cheques at once, with a Monthly series for a run of monthly cheques. They post under one number, -01, -02 …, and each then clears or bounces by itself. See Post-dated Cheques.
  • Create next, and linked documents. From a Sales Order, Delivery Note, invoice, requisition, Purchase Order or Goods Receipt, Create next starts the next document already filled in, and Linked documents lists the documents before and after it, each opened from its number. See Working with screens.

24/10/2026

  • Your own item codes. Give an item a User code in Item Master: it shows in place of the item code everywhere, and you can type it wherever you enter an item.
  • What reserved stock is for. In Stock Availability, select a Reserved quantity to see the Sales Orders reserving it, and open one from there.
  • New Ref shown in Allocate. When matching a payment to bills, and in Re-match, the part left over shows as its own New Ref line, and All as New Ref makes the whole line an advance. See Paying and allocating.
  • Proforma Invoice from a Sales Order. Print a saved Sales Order as a Proforma Invoice with its own PI number, the same on every reprint; a draft prints marked DRAFT. See Sales Order.
  • Choose your companies at the top. With more than one company, the button after the title chooses which of them the screens offer; with one chosen, they fill it in for you. Your choice is kept for the next sign-in. See Getting started.
  • Sign in with your user ID. On the sign-in page, enter your email or your user ID (your Username in User Management). Usernames are now unique in your organisation. See Getting started.
  • Default favourites per role. In Roles & Permissions, the star on a role sets up to 5 favourites for its users who haven't chosen their own. Nobody's own favourites are changed.
  • Light or dark, everywhere you sign in. Your choice of Light mode or Dark mode now goes with you to every computer. The ERP starts in light mode until you choose. See Getting started.
  • The menu by keyboard. F2 goes to Search modules, the arrow keys move through the menu, Enter opens a screen, and F1 opens Help. See Getting started.
  • Data Reset covers every module. It can now clear HR and payroll (employees, leave, payroll runs and payslips, settlements, loans, requests), Sales and Purchase Orders, Job Orders, vendors, customers, price lists, supporting documents, and a new Setup group. It refuses to clear a master still in use, and says what uses it. Clearing employees also removes their Employee Portal sign-ins. Clearing supporting documents moves their files to the storage's trash. See Data Reset.
  • Leaving Reasons are in HR Setup. The list moved from Payroll Setup to HR Setup, with the same reasons. Who may change it is now the HR Setup right. Employees choose from it as before.
  • An export invoice prints in the customer's currency only. A Customer Invoice in a foreign currency with no VAT no longer shows the rate and amounts in the default currency on its print. With VAT, they still show. See Customer Invoice.
  • **Chart of Accounts: a renumbered account's old code is in its details.** The list no longer shows "formerly …" under it; open the account (Edit) to see its former codes and until when. See Chart of Accounts.
  • More room on wide screens. Employees and Sales Order use the width of a wide monitor, and the Sales Order's item names have more space. Other screens keep the usual width.
  • Production Entry opens complete. An entry opened while the screen was still loading showed its items as "not found" and its Job Order as not linked. It now waits for the lists, and a posted entry's Job Order shows by its number even once it is complete. See Production Entry.
  • No error after going Back while a screen loads. Leaving a screen (for example PDC Received or PDC Issued) before it has finished loading, by Back or by opening another module, no longer shows an error message. See Working with screens.
  • Rejected documents leave your tasks when they should. A rejected document that you correct and post without approval (for example at a lower amount) no longer stays under Rejected in your Home tasks.
  • Close a rejection you will not resubmit. Choose Close on it in your Home tasks; an Admin can also close one in Approval Tracker. The rejection stays on record as "Closed, not resubmitted". See Approvals.

23/10/2026

  • Your company's name on Home without a reload. When a company is assigned to you, the title above the menu shows it as soon as you come back to Home. See Getting started.
  • A menu folder can no longer be linked to a module. In Tree Management, an item either opens a screen or holds sub-items, and a top-level item (an area of the menu) never opens a screen. A folder linked to a module by mistake now stays in the menu, with everything under it, so it can be put right.

22/10/2026

  • Support. Your Admin can send an issue (a bug, a question or a change you need) to the ERP's support team from Support, with up to 5 screenshots or files, and follow their answers there. What you send is shared with the support team: the screen asks you to confirm it each time.

19/10/2026

  • The Corporate ID field starts empty. On the sign-in page, Forgot Password and the Employee Portal, type your Corporate ID; your browser can save it and offer it next time.
  • Sign in by Corporate ID. The sign-in page now uses the Corporate ID to choose which organisation's ERP you sign in to. Forgot Password and the Employee Portal ask for the Corporate ID too. Nothing else changes when you sign in.

18/10/2026

  • Production and cheques post all at once. Posting a Production Entry now updates its Job Order lines and reserves what it made for their Sales Orders in the same step, and posting, clearing, bouncing or cancelling a post-dated cheque writes its GL entry and the cheque together. If anything stops the posting, nothing of it is saved.
  • Goods receipts and vendor invoices post all at once. Posting a GRN now updates the quantities received on its Purchase Orders in the same step, and posting a Vendor Invoice marks its GRNs in the same step. If anything stops the posting, nothing of it is saved, so you can correct it and post again.

17/10/2026

  • Renumbering an account updates the stock documents too. When you renumber an account in the Chart of Accounts, GRNs, issues, deliveries, returns and the other stock documents now show its new code as well, like every other document.
  • Deliveries and customer invoices post all at once. Posting a Delivery Note now updates its Sales Orders and reservations in the same step, and posting a Customer Invoice marks its deliveries in the same step. If anything stops the posting, nothing of it is saved, so you can correct it and post again.

16/10/2026

  • Sales reps in the Employee Portal. A Sales tab for employees the office makes sales reps (Sales Invoice Configuration → Sales reps): their customers with balances, orders at the price list sent to the office as draft Sales Orders, and cash or cheque collections recorded for the office to post. See Sales reps in the Employee Portal.
  • E-invoicing, ready. Switch it on per company in Sales Invoice Configuration, from a date: each posted Customer Invoice and Sales Return to a customer with a TRN gets its UAE e-invoice (PINT AE), checked, with its status and the XML to download. It is sent once your Accredited Service Provider is chosen and connected; nothing is sent before.
  • Projects, and which accounts need a cost center. A Project is a new kind of Organizational Unit, with a customer and dates; a Closed one takes no new postings. Journal and Cash and Bank lines take a project, and an invoice's project goes on all its lines. In the Chart of Accounts each heading (and any account) says whether its lines need a cost center, profit center and project: Required, Optional or Not used. Nothing changes until you set it: expenses still need a cost center. The new Project Report shows each project's income, costs and result; the GL Report, Trial Balance and P&L filter by project. See Organizational Units and Chart of Accounts.
  • Vendors and customers in a foreign currency. A vendor or customer whose Default Currency is, say, USD is invoiced, paid and given its statement in USD. Enter rates by date in **Currency Configuration → Rates** (1 USD = 3.6725 AED from a date until the next rate) and choose each company's Exchange Gain / Loss account. Customer and Vendor Invoices take the rate on their date (another rate needs a reason); in Cash and Bank you enter the USD amount beside the amount, and a difference from the bills' rates posts as an exchange gain or loss. Statements and aging show USD with the AED beside it. For now: USD invoices take direct lines (and item lines for customers), not GRNs or separate Delivery Notes. Journal Entries and Post-dated Cheques take the USD amount too, and Opening Balances take open documents in USD with their rate. A bank account can be in USD (Chart of Accounts → Currency): its vouchers are in USD and its ledger shows the USD balance. A printed USD invoice shows the VAT and the total in AED. See Currency Configuration, Customer Invoice, Vendor Invoice, Cash and Bank and Statements of account.
  • Item categories of your own. Besides the five, add a category such as Spare Parts with New category… in the item form. It has its own code prefix (SP-000001) and behaves like one of the five for BOMs and production. It can be shared or belong to one company, and can have its own sub-categories. See Item Master.
  • Chart of Accounts: classes, groups and renumbering. Codes now have a level each: a class per type (100000 Assets), groups under it (110000 Cash) and the accounts under a group (110001). An account can no longer sit straight under a class. Cash and bank accounts are the ones under a group marked Cash or Bank, not those with a certain code. Renumber gives a used account another code in its own group, and every entry and document follows. See Chart of Accounts.
  • Search any long list. Choosing an account, item, customer, vendor or a line's party now opens a searchable list. Type any part of the code or name ("trad" finds Beta Trading), choose with the arrow keys and Enter, and find your recent choices at the top. On a phone it fills the screen. See Working with screens.
  • End-of-service statement. A final settlement now prints an End-of-Service Settlement Statement for the employee (**Print statement**). It shows the service, the gratuity worked out by slab, entitlements, deductions and the net, in figures and in words. It is marked DRAFT until posted. Once the settlement is paid, the employee also finds it in the Employee Portal under My payslips. See Final Settlement.
  • Sell from stock on the invoice. Where a company switches it on (Sales Invoice Configuration → Invoices may deliver stock directly), a Customer Invoice can have item lines, typed or brought in **From Sales Order**. When it is posted, the invoice delivers them itself: a Delivery Note is made for them, the stock leaves at its cost and the order counts them as delivered. Print delivery slip prints the items without prices. See Customer Invoice.
  • Payroll: share an employee's cost across cost centers. A Transfer in Employee Change can now set a cost split by % (for example 60% / 40%). Every payroll expense of the employee is then shared that way. In a draft payroll run you can also change one employee's cost center (or split) for this run only, with a reason. The payslip now shows the cost center it posted to, even after a later transfer. See Employee Change and Payroll Run.
  • Paste lines from Excel into Purchase Requisition, Inventory Purchase Requisition and Sales Order: Copy Table gives the columns (also on an empty grid), Paste from Excel adds the rows. A row that isn't right stops the paste and is named. On a Sales Order the price list's price is used where the item has one. See Purchase Requisition, Inventory Purchase Requisition and Sales Order.
  • Fix a payment's matching without reversing it. A posted payment or receipt that settles the wrong bill: open it and select **Re-match references**, choose again, and give the reason. The books don't change, and it works in a closed month. It needs the new rematch right. See Paying and allocating.
  • Month-end checklist: a new check for **deliveries posted but not invoiced; your own accounts that must be zero** at month end (clearing accounts); and your own steps to tick before closing. See Period Closing.
  • Retail counter: find a customer by code, name or mobile number; an unknown mobile can become a new customer there. A mobile number now belongs to one customer of a company, also in Customer Master. See Retail Sales and Customer Master.
  • Shareholders in Company Master, with their % of the issued shares, their history and a printed company profile. They are seen with a new right, shareholders. See Company Master.
  • Tasks on Home: a posted delivery stays a task for the Invoicing team (or its maker) until it is invoiced; post-dated cheques coming due go to the Treasury team, 3 days before the cheque date (changeable). Both are set in Approval Configuration. See Approval Configuration.
  • Lists: choose how many rows a page holds. Journal entries, cash and bank, GRNs, purchase orders and the other document lists have Rows beside the count: 25, 50 or 100. Your choice is remembered in this browser for every list. See Working with screens.
  • Leaving reasons. Payroll Setup has a new Leaving Reasons tab (Resignation, Termination or Other), with common reasons to start and room for your own. An employee on notice or who has left must have one, with an optional note. See Payroll Setup and Employee Master.
  • A leaver's sign-in ends on its own. Someone leaving keeps the ERP and the Employee Portal until their final settlement is paid, and never more than 30 days after their last working day. HR can extend it, keep it, or go back to the rule, from the employee record. User Management shows "Leaving: access until …" or "Access ended". See Employee Master and User Management.
  • Employee Portal queries. An employee can now withdraw a payroll query (giving a reason) or mark it resolved, at any time while it is open. See Employee Requests.
  • Locations. Organizational Units has a new type, Location (an office, a site, a yard), shared by all companies or of one. Choose it on the employee record and on the fixed asset record; an asset's location can change at any time. See Organizational Units and Fixed Asset Master.
  • Item sub-categories by company. A sub-category can now be shared by all companies or belong to one. Item Master shows the shared ones and the item's company's, and New… beside Sub-Category opens Item Sub-Category to add one. See Item Sub-Category.
  • One print style. Printed documents (invoices, vouchers, orders, delivery notes and the like) now have a light, rounded frame and light lines between the cells. Payslips keep their own layout.

14/10/2026

  • User Management: two fixes. Adding a new user straight after editing another one no longer saves over the edited user. Last Login now shows when each user last signed in, in your local time. See User Management.
  • Retail Sales: the Items total is now the number of units on the sale; with a discount, the amount before it shows as Subtotal. See Retail Sales.
  • Chart of Accounts opens on Active accounts; choose All Statuses to see retired ones. See Chart of Accounts.
  • Approval Tracker can be filtered by status: Pending, Approved or Rejected. See Approval Tracker.
  • Inventory Aging: click an item code to open it in the Stock Ledger. See Inventory Aging.
  • Company Master lists companies in code order. See Company Master.
  • Help has a Light mode / Dark mode button, and the ERP's Help button no longer appears on prints. See Getting started.
