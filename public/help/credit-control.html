Credit Control

Each customer's credit limit and credit hold in each company, and what it owes and has coming against them.

Screen: Credit Control

What it is for

Credit Control keeps, for each company, a credit limit and, when needed, a credit hold for each customer, and shows where each customer stands: what it owes and has coming, against its limit, and what is overdue.

The screen has two tabs: Customers and Setup. Choose the company at the top. A customer shared by every company has a limit of its own in each.

Step by step

  1. In Setup, choose what counts against the limit and the grace days, and select Save.
  2. In Customers, type each customer's Credit limit and select Save limit.
  3. Watch the Standing: put a customer on hold when it must buy no more on credit, and lift it when settled.

The customers

Each customer of the company is listed, with:

ColumnWhat it shows
Credit limitIn the company's base currency. Blank: no limit. 0: cash only.
ReceivableWhat it owes: open invoices less credits not yet applied. Cheques in hand are still in it until they clear (shown under it).
Delivered, not invoicedPosted Delivery Notes not yet invoiced, at their prices before tax.
Open ordersConfirmed Sales Orders' quantities not yet delivered, at their prices before tax.
ExposureWhat counts against the limit, as Setup says.
AvailableThe limit less the exposure (red when over).
OverdueOpen invoices more than the grace days past their due date, and the oldest's days.
StandingOn hold, Over the limit, Overdue, or Good.

Customers on hold come first, then those over their limit, then the overdue, then the most exposed.

  • Select a customer's name to see its open items, deliveries not yet invoiced and open orders.
  • Only those on hold, over their limit or overdue narrows the list.
  • Copy copies the list for Excel.

Example: Example Retail LLC owes 1,000 (40 days overdue), has 200 delivered not yet invoiced and 300 on an open order: its exposure is 1,500. With a limit of 1,200, it is 300 over.

Setting a limit

Type the amount in Credit limit and select Save limit (or press Enter). Clear it and save for no limit. Each change is kept in the customer's history with who made it.

A credit hold

Put on hold asks for the reason (a dispute, a returned cheque), which is shown under the customer's name with who put it on. Lift hold takes it off, with a note if you wish. A hold applies whatever the limit.

Setup

  • The credit check: Off (to start), Warn or Block. The check on confirming a Sales Order and posting a Customer Invoice comes in the next step; until then the setting is kept and the list shows each customer's standing.
  • Counted against the limit: what the customer owes, deliveries not yet invoiced and open Sales Orders (to start); or what it owes only.
  • Overdue after: the days past the due date before an invoice counts as overdue (0: any day late).

Rights

In Roles & Permissions, Credit Control: access reads the list; write sets limits; hold puts on and lifts holds; setup changes Setup; override goes ahead where the credit check blocks (with the check, next).
