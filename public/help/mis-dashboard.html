MIS Dashboard

Your tiles of key figures, charts, top lists and gauges, worked out when you open it; your own, or your role's.

Screen: MIS Dashboard

What it is for

The MIS Dashboard shows the figures you watch most, as tiles, for the company and month you choose. Each tile is worked out when you open the dashboard, from the same reports as MIS Reports, so it is always up to date.

Before you start

  • You need access to the MIS Dashboard, and to MIS Reports for the figures.
  • To change your own tiles you need the MIS design right; to set a role's dashboard, the MIS publish right (Admin and Finance Manager by default).

The tiles

  • Key figure: one figure, for example Sales 12,400.00, with its change against the comparison and the %, green when the change is good (more sales, fewer expenses) and red when not.
  • Chart: a report as bars or a line, for example the 12-month trend. Months read Nov-2026.
  • Top list: the five largest lines of a report, for example the largest receivables.
  • Gauge: a figure against the good band you set, for example a current ratio between 1.00 and 2.00. Inside the band it shows green, outside amber. "No figure for this period" means the report has nothing to measure yet (for a current ratio: no current liabilities in the MIS setup).

Which dashboard you see

  1. Your own, once you have saved one.
  2. Otherwise your role's, if someone set one for it.
  3. Otherwise the starter dashboard: sales, net profit, bank and cash, receivables, the 12-month trend, the largest receivables and the current ratio.

Step by step

  1. Open MIS Dashboard and choose the company and the month.
  2. Edit to change the tiles: choose the tile, the report, the figure (for a key figure or gauge), the comparison, the chart style or the good band, and a title, then Add tile. Remove a tile with ×, move it with the arrows.
  3. Save my dashboard. Reset removes your own tiles and shows your role's dashboard again.
  4. To set a role's dashboard (MIS publish): while editing, choose the role and Make default. Its users who have not made their own see it. Remove the role's default takes it away.

A report you built and saved (Build a report) can be a chart or a top list: choose it under My built reports.

Show on Home

Tick Show on Home to see your tiles at the top of Home too, with their company and Open MIS Dashboard. It is your own choice and off until you tick it.

Questions and mistakes

  • A tile says it cannot load. You may not have access to MIS Reports, or the report does not fit the comparison (the Balance Sheet has no budget).
  • Charts could not load. Reload the page; the charts come with the app.
