GL Account Reconciliation

Explain the balance of each balance-sheet account the company reconciles monthly, by matching its lines or by a schedule, approved and locked month by month.

Screen: GL Account Reconciliation

What it is for

Some accounts should hold only what is still open: GRN Clearing (goods received, not yet billed), suspense, advances, accruals, prepayments. Each month their balance is explained item by item, and approved, so anything stuck or wrong is found while it is fresh.

The screen has three tabs: The month, Reconcile and Setup. Choose the company and the month at the top.

Step by step

  1. In Setup, tick the accounts reconciled each month and choose how (for those with the setup right):
    • Match lines: for clearing and suspense accounts. Lines that offset each other are matched; what stays unmatched is the balance.
    • Schedule: for accruals and prepayments. A list of items that make up the balance.

Bank accounts are reconciled in Bank Reconciliation.

  1. The month shows each listed account and bank account with its status for the month. Select an account to reconcile it.
  2. Reconcile it:
    • Match lines: tick lines that offset each other (a GRN and its bill, an advance and its settlement). The selection shows when they net to nothing. Then select Match the lines selected. Match 1 ✕ undoes a match.
    • Schedule: Add an item for each part of the balance (a debit as a positive amount, a credit as negative), with a reference if you wish, then Save the schedule. Attach the support (a statement, a calculation) under Supporting documents.
  3. When the Difference is 0.00, select Submit. It goes to the approvers set in Approval Configuration for GL Account Reconciliation (with none set, it is approved at once). Once approved, the month is locked.

Example: Example Trading LLC's GRN Clearing has a credit balance of 300.00. A GRN of 500.00 is matched with its bill. The GRN of 300.00 still waiting for its bill is the balance, and it carries into next month until its bill comes.

Locking and reopening

  • A month can be submitted only when its difference is nothing and the account's previous month is approved: the months go in order.
  • Someone with the reopen right can Reopen a locked month, giving a reason (kept in its History). Only the latest locked month of an account can be reopened.
  • A rejected month shows the reason; change it and submit again.
  • Period Closing checks that each listed account has its month approved (Period Closing).

Rights

In Roles & Permissions, GL Account Reconciliation: access reads; prepare matches lines, keeps the schedule and notes, submits and deletes a draft; reopen reopens a locked month; setup chooses the accounts and their methods. Approving follows the approval levels.
