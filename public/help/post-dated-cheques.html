Post-dated Cheques (PDC Received and Issued)

Cheques dated in the future: received from customers or issued to vendors, then cleared, bounced or cancelled.

Screens: PDC Received, PDC Issued

What it is for

A post-dated cheque (PDC) settles invoices on the day you receive or issue it, but the money only moves on the cheque date. These screens keep the cheque in a clearing account until then.

  • PDC Received: cheques from customers.
  • PDC Issued: cheques you give vendors.

Before you start

  • In Company Master, the company's PDC Received Clearing and PDC Issued Clearing accounts.
  • The customer or vendor set up with its receivables / payables account.

Step by step

Record a cheque, or a set of cheques

  1. Select New PDC.
  2. Choose the Company and the customer or vendor.
  3. Enter the date received or issued and a description.
    • Issued: choose your bank account the cheques are drawn on.
  4. Under Cheques, enter each cheque's Cheque No., Cheque Date and amount.
    • Received: type the Drawee Bank (the customer's bank) for each.
    • Add cheque adds a line.
    • Monthly series adds more cheques after the last one, a month apart, with the next numbers and the same amount and bank. For example, cheque 000101 dated 31/01/2026 with two more gives 000102 on 28/02/2026 and 000103 on 31/03/2026. Change any line afterwards.
  5. Allocate the total to the invoices it pays (or Oldest first). Anything not allocated stays as an advance. With several cheques, the earliest cheque settles the oldest invoice, and so on.
  6. If it replaces a bounced cheque, choose it in Replaces.
  7. Save Draft, or Post (or submit for approval, where set up).

Each cheque after posting

A set of cheques is posted as one entry with one number. Each cheque gets that number with -01, -02 … (the earliest cheque first) and its own GL entry. From then on each cheque is cleared, bounced or cancelled on its own.

Open any of them to see the whole entry: every cheque with its status (for example "1 of 3 cleared"); select another to work on it. Print gives one receipt for the entry, listing all its cheques.

Clear it on its date

When the cheque date comes, the cheque appears in Home tasks under Post-dated Cheques Due to Clear and in the Due to clear view. If a Treasury team is set for the company in Approval Configuration, only the team sees it on Home, and a few days before its date (3 to start); a cheque not yet due shows its date in amber, an overdue one in red. Select Clear to Bank with the clearing date (not before the cheque date); for a received cheque, choose the bank account it was deposited into. Several cheques can be cleared together with Clear Selected.

Cheques a sales rep collected

A cheque a rep collected in the Employee Portal (see Sales reps) is posted at once, with its receipt, and marked with the rep in the list. When the rep hands it over, open it and choose Received from the rep (or tick several and Received from the rep above the list): no money moves, and the cheque can then be cleared. Clear to Bank and Bounced wait until then.

If it doesn't go through

  • Bounced: the bank returned it. Enter the date and reason; the invoices it settled are open again (only what this cheque settled, for one of a set).
  • Cancel Cheque: withdrawn before clearing, same effect.
  • Undo Clearing: a clearing entered by mistake goes back to Pending.

Bank charges for a bounced cheque are entered separately (a JV or CBT).

From an invoice's Linked documents, Create next opens a new one here already started for the invoice's open amount. See Working with screens.

What happens in the books

EventPDC ReceivedPDC Issued
PostDr PDC Received Clearing · Cr the customerDr the vendor · Cr PDC Issued Clearing
ClearDr Bank · Cr PDC Received ClearingDr PDC Issued Clearing · Cr Bank
Bounce / cancelThe post entry reversed: the party owes againThe same

The invoices show "Paid (PDC pending)" until the cheque clears. Statements of account list pending cheques separately.

Rights

As Cash and Bank Transactions: view, draft, and post (which also covers clear, bounce, cancel and undo).

Questions and mistakes

  • Refused as a duplicate: the same cheque number (and bank, and party) is already recorded and not bounced or cancelled.
  • Can't clear before the cheque date.
  • Can't bounce: an advance the cheque created has since been used against an invoice; undo that first.
  • "… is invoiced in USD: enter the cheque's USD amount too": the party is in a foreign currency. Enter the cheque's Amount (what the bank pays) and its USD amount: the cheque settles bills in USD, and a difference from their rates posts as an exchange gain or loss. A bounce or cancellation undoes both.
  • A draft made by "Portal: …" is a cheque a sales rep collected in the Employee Portal, with its photo in the supporting documents (Sales reps): check it and post it.
