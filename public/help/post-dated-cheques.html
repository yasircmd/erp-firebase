Post-dated Cheques (PDC Received and Issued)

Cheques dated in the future: received from customers or issued to vendors, then cleared, bounced or cancelled.

Screens: PDC Received, PDC Issued

What it is for

A post-dated cheque (PDC) settles invoices on the day you receive or issue it, but the money only moves on the cheque date. These screens keep the cheque in a clearing account until then.

  • PDC Received: cheques from customers.
  • PDC Issued: cheques you give vendors.

Before you start

  • In Company Master, the company's PDC Received Clearing and PDC Issued Clearing accounts.
  • The customer or vendor set up with its receivables / payables account.

Step by step

Record a cheque

  1. Select New PDC.
  2. Choose the Company and the customer or vendor.
  3. Enter the date received or issued, the Cheque Date, Cheque No., the amount and a description.
    • Received: type the Drawee Bank (the customer's bank).
    • Issued: choose your bank account it is drawn on.
  4. Allocate the cheque to the invoices it pays (or Oldest first). Anything not allocated stays as an advance.
  5. If it replaces a bounced cheque, choose it in Replaces.
  6. Save Draft, or Post (or submit for approval, where set up).

Clear it on its date

When the cheque date comes, the cheque appears in Home tasks under Post-dated Cheques Due to Clear and in the Due to clear view. Select Clear to Bank with the clearing date (not before the cheque date); for a received cheque, choose the bank account it was deposited into. Several cheques can be cleared together with Clear Selected.

If it doesn't go through

  • Bounced: the bank returned it. Enter the date and reason; the invoices it settled are open again.
  • Cancel Cheque: withdrawn before clearing, same effect.
  • Undo Clearing: a clearing entered by mistake goes back to Pending.

Bank charges for a bounced cheque are entered separately (a JV or CBT).

What happens in the books

EventPDC ReceivedPDC Issued
PostDr PDC Received Clearing · Cr the customerDr the vendor · Cr PDC Issued Clearing
ClearDr Bank · Cr PDC Received ClearingDr PDC Issued Clearing · Cr Bank
Bounce / cancelThe post entry reversed: the party owes againThe same

The invoices show "Paid (PDC pending)" until the cheque clears. Statements of account list pending cheques separately.

Rights

As Cash and Bank Transactions: view, draft, and post (which also covers clear, bounce, cancel and undo).

Questions and mistakes

  • Refused as a duplicate: the same cheque number (and bank, and party) is already recorded and not bounced or cancelled.
  • Can't clear before the cheque date.
  • Can't bounce: an advance the cheque created has since been used against an invoice; undo that first.
