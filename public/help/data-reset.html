Data Reset

Clear test data module by module, or rebuild the stock balances, before going live.

Screen: Data Reset (Admin)

What it is for

Data Reset deletes data, for a fresh start while setting up: the test entries made while learning the ERP, or the masters and setup to enter again. It deletes for good: there is no undo. Never use it on live records.

It works in your corporate only: a reset never touches another corporate's data.

Before you start

  • You are signed in with the ADMIN role: no other role can open it.
  • You know which categories to clear. Nothing is ticked for you.
  • To clear supporting documents, the document storage is connected (Document Storage).

The categories

  • Transactional data: what accumulates from use. GL entries; stock transactions with the stock balances and reservations; vendor and customer invoices; PDCs; fixed assets; recurring journals; opening balances; Sales Orders; Purchase Requisitions and Orders; Job Orders; approval records; e-invoices; employee changes; leave; payroll runs and payslips; final settlements; payroll loans; employee requests; budgets; supporting documents; company documents; period closing; the logs.
  • Master data: items and their categories, sub-categories, units and barcodes; the chart of accounts; companies; org units; sales types; customer classifications; BOMs; vendors; customers; price lists; the inventory valuation list; requisition categories; currencies and exchange rates; shareholders; employees; credit limits and holds.
  • Setup: HR Setup, Payroll Setup (with the employee request types), Approval Configuration, Tax Configuration, Budget Setup, Credit Control Setup, and the numbering formats and settings (with the Rep Cash Clearing account).

Budgets (a transaction) are every version of every company's budget, and every forecast; the chart of accounts and the org units can't be cleared while budgets use them.

Sales Rep Receipts (a transaction) are the receipts the reps gave from the Employee Portal; each rep's numbers restart. Their GL entries and cheques are cleared with the GL and PDCs.

Users, roles, permissions and the menu are never offered.

Step by step

  1. Open Data Reset. Each category shows how many records it has.
  2. To delete a whole category, tick it. To delete some records only, use Browse & select… on its row (where offered).
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

What it refuses, and what goes with a category

  • A master still in use is refused. For example, Employees while payroll runs exist, or Customers while invoices exist. The message names what still uses it and how many records it has. Tick those too, or clear them first. Nothing is deleted until the whole selection may go.
  • A master that others use is cleared whole, never one record at a time.
  • Clearing a category restarts its numbering (for example, employee numbers or vendor codes).
  • Employees also removes their Employee Portal sign-ins, but never an ERP user's own sign-in.
  • Supporting Documents also moves the files to the document storage's trash. Google Drive keeps them there for 30 days.
  • A payroll run goes with its payslips, and a Sales Order with its reservations.

Recalculate Stock Ledger

After deleting single stock transactions, the stock balances may no longer match them. Recalculate Stock Ledger rebuilds the balances from the posted transactions that remain.

Rights

Only the ADMIN role, whatever Roles & Permissions say.

Questions and mistakes

  • "… is still used by …": clear what it names first, or with it.
  • "… not connected": connect the document storage in Document Storage, then clear Supporting Documents again.
  • Deleted the wrong thing: there is no undo. Enter the data again, or restore from your own backup.
