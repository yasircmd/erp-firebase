Build a report

Your own report from sales, purchase, GL or stock lines, or a built module's records: filtered, grouped with totals, as a pivot or a list; saved and shared.

What it is for

Build a report makes a report the catalogue does not have, from the lines of a data set: for example sales by rep and month, purchases by vendor, or stock movements by warehouse.

Before you start

  • MIS Reports access, and the data set's own module:
    • Sales lines: Customer Invoice;
    • Purchase lines: Vendor Invoice;
    • GL lines: General Ledger;
    • Stock movements: Stock Ledger;
    • a built module: that module.

The data sets

  • Sales lines: each invoice line, retail sale, and sales or retail return (returns negative): customer, rep, item, category, account, sales type, cost center, project, quantity, amount.
  • Purchase lines: each vendor invoice line: vendor, item, category, account, cost center, project, quantity, amount.
  • GL lines: each posted journal line: voucher type, account and its type, party, cost and profit center, project, debit, credit, and debit − credit.
  • Stock movements: each stock line as the Stock Ledger shows it: type, in or out, item, category, warehouse, quantity and value (in +, out −).

Sales lines and Purchase lines also have a Variant column (for example Room Invoice, blank for an ordinary invoice) and each variant's extra fields (for example Room Invoice · Room), so a variant's revenue can be shown on its own.

Built modules

Under Built modules in the data set list, each of your built modules is a data set of its own:

  • A master (for example Rooms): one row per record, as it is today, whatever months are chosen: its code, name, whether it is switched off, and its fields.
  • A document (for example Stays): one row per document dated in the months chosen: its number, date, month, status, fields, and the totals of its lines.
  • A document with lines is also a second data set (for example Stay – rooms): one row per line, the document's own fields repeated on each.

A field that links to another built master brings that master's fields too, for example a stay's room brings Room · Room type, so bookings can be grouped by room type. A calculated field (for example Nights or Net due) is a number like any amount. Long text fields are not in reports.

Amounts are in the base currency, before tax, after each document's discount. In GL lines a discount is its own line, so an invoice's revenue there is the full amount.

Step by step

  1. In MIS Reports, choose Build a report… in the report list.
  2. Choose the data set and the months (1 to 24, ending at the month chosen).
  3. Choose how to show it:
    • Grouped with totals: group by one field, then optionally a second (for example rep, then month), with one or two totals (total, count of lines, average, lowest, highest).
    • Pivot: rows (for example customer), columns (for example month) and the figure. At most the 24 busiest columns show.
    • A list of lines: tick the columns.
  4. Add a filter as needed: a field, then is, is not, contains, after / over, before / under or between, and the value. For a date, is 2026-11 means that month.
  5. Show. Click a column's heading to sort by it.
  6. Save as… keeps it in My reports; share it and put it on the MIS Dashboard as a chart or a top list.

Questions and mistakes

  • Too many lines. Choose fewer months or add a filter.
  • A built module is missing from the list. It is not published yet, it is a variant (its documents are in Sales or Purchase lines), or you do not have its access right.
  • "is not a number: count it instead". Totals need a number field; for any other field choose Count of lines.
