What's new (page 3)

The latest changes to the ERP, newest first, with a link to each screen's page.

← Newer changes · Page 3 of 5 · Older changes →

The latest changes, newest first. Each links to the page that explains the screen.

25/10/2026

  • Request types of your own. In Employee Requests HR sets up the requests employees raise from the portal: a salary certificate, IT support … each with its own form, straight to its handler or through the line manager first, an optional time to answer (overdue shown), and a document as the answer. Employees find them under Requests → Other; numbered RQ-.
  • Leaver clearance. The new Clearances screen takes each leaver through the company's steps (Finance, IT, HR by default), each signed off by its team, with a checklist and any amount to recover. The final settlement goes for approval once every step is signed; the leaver follows it in the Employee Portal.
  • Hold a salary. Payroll's new Salary holds tab stops an employee's salary from a month: the payslip shows a Stop Salary deduction and the amount waits on a Salary held account (set it in Payroll Setup). Released, it is paid with the next run. See Payroll.
  • Several post-dated cheques in one entry. Enter a customer's or vendor's set of cheques at once, with a Monthly series for a run of monthly cheques. They post under one number, -01, -02 …, and each then clears or bounces by itself. See Post-dated Cheques.
  • Create next, and linked documents. From a Sales Order, Delivery Note, invoice, requisition, Purchase Order or Goods Receipt, Create next starts the next document already filled in, and Linked documents lists the documents before and after it, each opened from its number. See Working with screens.

24/10/2026

  • Your own item codes. Give an item a User code in Item Master: it shows in place of the item code everywhere, and you can type it wherever you enter an item.
  • What reserved stock is for. In Stock Availability, select a Reserved quantity to see the Sales Orders reserving it, and open one from there.
  • New Ref shown in Allocate. When matching a payment to bills, and in Re-match, the part left over shows as its own New Ref line, and All as New Ref makes the whole line an advance. See Paying and allocating.
  • Proforma Invoice from a Sales Order. Print a saved Sales Order as a Proforma Invoice with its own PI number, the same on every reprint; a draft prints marked DRAFT. See Sales Order.
  • Choose your companies at the top. With more than one company, the button after the title chooses which of them the screens offer; with one chosen, they fill it in for you. Your choice is kept for the next sign-in. See Getting started.
  • Sign in with your user ID. On the sign-in page, enter your email or your user ID (your Username in User Management). Usernames are now unique in your organisation. See Getting started.
  • Default favourites per role. In Roles & Permissions, the star on a role sets up to 5 favourites for its users who haven't chosen their own. Nobody's own favourites are changed.
  • Light or dark, everywhere you sign in. Your choice of Light mode or Dark mode now goes with you to every computer. The ERP starts in light mode until you choose. See Getting started.
  • The menu by keyboard. F2 goes to Search modules, the arrow keys move through the menu, Enter opens a screen, and F1 opens Help. See Getting started.
  • Data Reset covers every module. It can now clear HR and payroll (employees, leave, payroll runs and payslips, settlements, loans, requests), Sales and Purchase Orders, Job Orders, vendors, customers, price lists, supporting documents, and a new Setup group. It refuses to clear a master still in use, and says what uses it. Clearing employees also removes their Employee Portal sign-ins. Clearing supporting documents moves their files to the storage's trash. See Data Reset.
  • Leaving Reasons are in HR Setup. The list moved from Payroll Setup to HR Setup, with the same reasons. Who may change it is now the HR Setup right. Employees choose from it as before.
  • An export invoice prints in the customer's currency only. A Customer Invoice in a foreign currency with no VAT no longer shows the rate and amounts in the default currency on its print. With VAT, they still show. See Customer Invoice.
  • Chart of Accounts: a renumbered account's old code is in its details. The list no longer shows "formerly …" under it; open the account (Edit) to see its former codes and until when. See Chart of Accounts.
  • More room on wide screens. Employees and Sales Order use the width of a wide monitor, and the Sales Order's item names have more space. Other screens keep the usual width.
  • Production Entry opens complete. An entry opened while the screen was still loading showed its items as "not found" and its Job Order as not linked. It now waits for the lists, and a posted entry's Job Order shows by its number even once it is complete. See Production Entry.
  • No error after going Back while a screen loads. Leaving a screen (for example PDC Received or PDC Issued) before it has finished loading, by Back or by opening another module, no longer shows an error message. See Working with screens.
  • Rejected documents leave your tasks when they should. A rejected document that you correct and post without approval (for example at a lower amount) no longer stays under Rejected in your Home tasks.
  • Close a rejection you will not resubmit. Choose Close on it in your Home tasks; an Admin can also close one in Approval Tracker. The rejection stays on record as "Closed, not resubmitted". See Approvals.

23/10/2026

  • Your company's name on Home without a reload. When a company is assigned to you, the title above the menu shows it as soon as you come back to Home. See Getting started.
  • A menu folder can no longer be linked to a module. In Tree Management, an item either opens a screen or holds sub-items, and a top-level item (an area of the menu) never opens a screen. A folder linked to a module by mistake now stays in the menu, with everything under it, so it can be put right.

22/10/2026

  • Support. Your Admin can send an issue (a bug, a question or a change you need) to the ERP's support team from Support, with up to 5 screenshots or files, and follow their answers there. What you send is shared with the support team: the screen asks you to confirm it each time.

19/10/2026

  • The Corporate ID field starts empty. On the sign-in page, Forgot Password and the Employee Portal, type your Corporate ID; your browser can save it and offer it next time.
  • Sign in by Corporate ID. The sign-in page now uses the Corporate ID to choose which organisation's ERP you sign in to. Forgot Password and the Employee Portal ask for the Corporate ID too. Nothing else changes when you sign in.

18/10/2026

  • Production and cheques post all at once. Posting a Production Entry now updates its Job Order lines and reserves what it made for their Sales Orders in the same step, and posting, clearing, bouncing or cancelling a post-dated cheque writes its GL entry and the cheque together. If anything stops the posting, nothing of it is saved.
  • Goods receipts and vendor invoices post all at once. Posting a GRN now updates the quantities received on its Purchase Orders in the same step, and posting a Vendor Invoice marks its GRNs in the same step. If anything stops the posting, nothing of it is saved, so you can correct it and post again.
