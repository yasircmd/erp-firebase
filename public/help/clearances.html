Clearances

A leaver's clearance, step by step (Finance, IT, HR …), before the final settlement goes for approval.

Screen: Clearances

What it is for

Before an employee leaves, each department clears them: Finance checks loans and advances, IT collects the laptop and closes the accounts, HR takes back the documents. Clearances tracks those steps for every employee on notice or who has left, until their final settlement is posted. The final settlement can't be submitted for approval until every step is signed off.

Before you start

  • Set-up (needs the Clearances setup right): open Clearances, choose the company and select Set-up.
    • The steps, in the order they run. Each has its name, its team (the users who sign it off; they need Clearances access) and its checklist, one item a line.
    • Whether the steps run in order (IT opens once Finance has signed) or all at once.
    • The deduction pay element an amount to recover posts through (for example "Assets not returned"), if steps may enter one.
  • A company with no set-up of its own uses Finance → IT → HR, with a short checklist each. Saving no steps at all switches clearance off for the company: its settlements then don't wait for one.
  • A change to the set-up applies to clearances started from then on.

Step by step

A clearance starts

When an employee is put On notice (or marked Left) with a leaving date in Employees, their clearance starts with the company's steps. It also starts for a leaver whose final settlement is not yet posted, the first time anyone opens Clearances. If the employee becomes Active again before anyone signed, the clearance goes.

Sign off a step

  1. The step's team sees it on Home under Clearances Waiting for You, or opens Clearances (Waiting for my team).
  2. Open the employee. Tick each item done, and write any notes.
  3. If something wasn't returned, enter the amount to recover and what it is for. For example, 1,500.00 for "laptop not returned".
  4. Save keeps your work; Sign off completes the step. In order, the next step then opens for its team.

A step with no team is signed off by users with the Clearances manage right, who may also sign off any step. Reopen (manage) takes a signed step back, with a reason, until the final settlement is posted.

The final settlement

Once every step is signed off, the clearance is Complete and the final settlement can be submitted. Each amount to recover is already on it as a deduction line ("Clearance: IT"), which HR can change there with a reason.

What the employee sees

In the Employee Portal, the leaver's Home shows Your clearance before leaving: each step (done, in progress or waiting) and what is still open in it (for example "Laptop returned"). They never see the notes or the amounts.

Rights

access sees the company's clearances and lets a team member sign off their own step; manage signs off any step and reopens; setup sets the steps.
