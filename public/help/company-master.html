Company Master

Keep the company profile, chart links and the legal defaults used across the ERP.

Screen: Company Master Data

What it is for

Company Master holds the company profile and many of the legal and operational defaults that the rest of the ERP relies on, including account structure and tax-related settings.

Before you start

  • The legal company information is agreed.
  • The financial account and tax configuration is ready.
  • The business knows which setup defaults must be shared across all modules.

Step by step

  1. Open Company Master and select the company. Companies are listed in code order (01, 02 … 10).
  2. Review the company information and operational defaults.
  3. Set the required account links and the fields that should be shared with all modules.
  4. Save and test a standard transaction against the company setup.

Shareholders

Select Shareholders (the people icon) on a company to see and keep its shareholders: name, nationality, ID or passport number, shares held, and the dates they held them from and to.

  • Enter the company's Issued shares: each shareholder's % is worked out of them. If the current shareholders don't hold exactly the issued shares (100%), a warning says so; you can still save, for example while a transfer is being entered.
  • A shareholder who sold or left: edit them and enter the To date. They stay on the list as a past shareholder, so the history is kept. A record entered by mistake can be removed, with a reason.
  • Changes lists every change: who made it and when.
  • Print company profile prints the company's details with its current shareholders and their %.

What happens in the books

Company Master is a setup record and not a posting document by itself. It provides the default company rules used by many other screens.

Rights

Usually restricted to ERP admin or finance-support roles.

Shareholders are personal data, so they have their own right: Company Master shareholders in Roles & Permissions, to see them. Changing them also needs Company Master's write right.

Questions and mistakes

  • If a screen cannot find the company or accounts, the company profile may not be set correctly.
