Working with screens

What every screen has in common: companies, dates, drafts and posting, messages, Copy to Excel, printing, attachments and closed months.

Company first

The ERP holds several companies. Most screens start with a Company choice; you see only the companies assigned to you. When you have only one, it is chosen for you. Lists, numbers and reports always belong to the company chosen.

Dates and periods

  • Dates show as dd/mm/yyyy (31/12/2026) and months as Sep-2026, everywhere: on screen, in print and in what you copy to Excel.
  • To enter a date, use the date field's calendar.

Drafts, submitting and posting

Entry screens (journals, invoices, receipts and so on) follow the same steps:

  1. New opens an empty document. Fill the header (company, date, party) and the lines.
  2. Save Draft keeps your work without any effect on the books or stock. Drafts appear in Home tasks under My Drafts, and you can change or delete them.
  3. Submit or Post:
    • where an approval is set up for that document, Submit sends it to the approver (see Approvals);
    • otherwise Post records it: the accounting entry and any stock movement are made, and the document gets its number.
  4. A posted document cannot be edited. To correct it, Reverse it (a mirror entry, dated in an open month) and enter it again.

The list on each entry screen shows documents by status; search and open any of them.

Messages and questions

Messages, confirmations ("Post this document?") and questions (for example a reason for a reversal) appear in a box inside the ERP. Read them before choosing: Cancel always leaves things as they were.

Copy to Excel and printing

  • Copy (on lists and reports) copies the table; paste it into Excel and each value lands in its own cell.
  • Print prints the report or document without the menus and buttons. To keep a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

Supporting documents

Entry screens have a Supporting documents section: Add files to keep the vendor's bill, a delivery note or a receipt with the document. Files are stored in the company's document storage, and anyone who can open the document can open its files.

Closed months

Finance closes each month after its checks (Period Closing). Once a month is closed, nothing can be posted, reversed or approved with a date in it; the ERP says which month is closed. Use a date in an open month, or ask someone with the reopen right to reopen the month.

Drilling down and going back

Reports link to their detail (a Trial Balance line to its ledger, a ledger line to its document). Use Back to … on the opened screen to return to the report where you left it.

Saving your place

The ERP signs you out after 10 minutes without activity (with a warning). Save drafts as you go on long entries.
