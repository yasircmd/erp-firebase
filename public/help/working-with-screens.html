Working with screens

What every screen has in common: companies, dates, drafts and posting, messages, Copy to Excel, printing, attachments and closed months.

Company first

The ERP holds several companies. Most screens start with a Company choice; you see only the companies assigned to you, and of those only the ones you chose at the top of Home (see Choosing your companies). When you have only one, or chose one, it is chosen for you. Lists, numbers and reports always belong to the company chosen.

Item codes

An item with a user code of your own shows it in place of its item code on every screen, print and copy, and you can type either where you enter an item. See Item Master.

Dates and periods

  • Dates show as dd/mm/yyyy (31/12/2026) and months as Sep-2026, everywhere: on screen, in print and in what you copy to Excel.
  • To enter a date, use the date field's calendar.

Drafts, submitting and posting

Entry screens (journals, invoices, receipts and so on) follow the same steps:

  1. New opens an empty document. Fill the header (company, date, party) and the lines.
  2. Save Draft keeps your work without any effect on the books or stock. Drafts appear in Home tasks under My Drafts, and you can change or delete them.
  3. Submit or Post:
    • where an approval is set up for that document, Submit sends it to the approver (see Approvals);
    • otherwise Post records it: the accounting entry and any stock movement are made, and the document gets its number.
  4. A posted document cannot be edited. To correct it, Reverse it (a mirror entry, dated in an open month) and enter it again.

The list on each entry screen shows documents by status; search and open any of them.

Finding an account, item, customer or vendor

Wherever you choose an account, an item, a customer, a vendor or another entry from a long list, you can search it:

  • In a code box (the account on a journal line, the item on a GRN line), start typing. The matches show under the box straight away. Type any part of the code or the name: "trad" finds both Trade Payables and Beta Trading. Codes that start with what you typed come first, then names. Choose with the mouse, or with the arrow keys and Enter (after the arrow keys, Tab also chooses). Esc closes the list and keeps what you typed.
  • In a long drop-down (a customer or vendor on a document, a journal line's party), click it or start typing a letter: a search box opens above the list.
  • The last five entries you chose from each list appear first, under Recent. They are kept in this browser.
  • On a phone the list fills the screen, with a box to type in.

Short drop-downs (a status, a company) work as before. The list offers the same entries as before: for example, a journal offers only the company's postable accounts.

Messages and questions

Messages, confirmations ("Post this document?") and questions (for example a reason for a reversal) appear in a box inside the ERP. Read them before choosing: Cancel always leaves things as they were.

Long lists

Document lists (journal entries, cash and bank, GRNs, purchase orders and others) show the newest entries first, a page at a time. Load next adds the following page below. Searching and filtering look through every entry, not only the ones shown. Rows beside the count sets how many a page holds: 25, 50 or 100. The choice is remembered in this browser for every list.

Copy to Excel and printing

  • Copy (on lists and reports) copies the table; paste it into Excel and each value lands in its own cell.
  • Entry screens with lines (journal entries, cash and bank, GRNs, the requisitions, sales orders and others) have Copy Table and Paste from Excel: copy the lines with their headings, fill them in Excel and paste them back. The headings row is skipped, and a paste with a wrong row is refused whole, naming the rows to fix.
  • Print prints the report or document without the menus and buttons. To keep a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
  • Printed documents (invoices, vouchers, orders, delivery notes and the like) share one look: their tables have a light, rounded frame and light lines between the cells. Payslips keep their own layout.

Linked documents and Create next

On the documents of a sale or a purchase (requisitions, Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts, Vendor Invoices, Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, Customer Invoices, and the payments and receipts that settle them), a Linked documents section sits just above the supporting documents:

  • Create next has a button for each step the document can still be followed by, for example Delivery Note on an approved Sales Order with quantity still to deliver, or Receipt and PDC Received on an invoice with an amount still open (shown beside them). The button opens the next screen with a new document already started: the company, the customer or vendor, and this document chosen. Check it, change what you need (for example the quantities, or Cash instead of Bank), then save it as usual. Nothing is saved until you do.
  • Comes from and Followed by list the documents before and after it, for example on an invoice its Sales Order and Delivery Notes, and the receipts, cheques and credit notes settling it. Select a number to open that document.

You see a button or a link only for the screens you may open (and, for Create next, create documents in). When a document is fully delivered, received, invoiced or paid, its Create next buttons go.

Supporting documents

Entry screens have a Supporting documents section: Add files to keep the vendor's bill, a delivery note or a receipt with the document. Files are stored in the company's document storage, and anyone who can open the document can open its files.

Closed months

Finance closes each month after its checks (Period Closing). Once a month is closed, nothing can be posted, reversed or approved with a date in it; the ERP says which month is closed. Use a date in an open month, or ask someone with the reopen right to reopen the month.

Drilling down and going back

Reports link to their detail (a Trial Balance line to its ledger, a ledger line to its document). Use Back to … on the opened screen to return to the report where you left it.

You can leave a screen at any time, even while it is still loading: what it was loading is dropped, and nothing on it is saved unless you saved it.

Saving your place

The ERP signs you out after 10 minutes without activity (with a warning). Save drafts as you go on long entries.
