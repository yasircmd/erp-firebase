Quotations

Price offers to customers and leads, with discounts and tax, approval, revisions, the print and Create Sales Order.

Screen: Quotation

What it is for

A quotation is the price you offer a customer, or a lead who is not a customer yet, before they order. It has item lines like a Sales Order's: the price from the customer's price list, a discount, and the items' tax. It posts nothing. When the customer accepts, Create Sales Order turns it into a draft order.

Choose the company at the top. The list shows the open quotations; choose another Status to see the rest.

Step by step

  1. Select New Quotation and choose the Customer or lead. For a lead, choose the Classification whose price list applies.
  2. Check the Date and Valid until (the CRM Setup's number of days on, 30 by default) and, if you like, the Opportunity it is for.
  3. Add the lines: type or choose the item; its price comes from the price list (or type it). Enter the Qty and any Disc %.
  4. Write the Terms and notes to print, and select Save. The quotation gets its number and the tax is worked out.
  5. Select Submit. Where Approval Configuration has levels for Quotation, it waits for approval; otherwise it is Approved at once.
  6. Print it (or save it as a PDF) and send it to the customer.
  7. When the customer accepts, select Create Sales Order.

Prices, discount and tax

  • The price list is the customer's (by their classification), or for a lead the classification you choose. Its currency is the quotation's.
  • Disc % takes the discount off the unit price; Amount is the net price times the quantity.
  • The tax is the same as a Sales Order's, by each item's tax code (see Tax Configuration). It shows once you save.

Statuses

  • Draft: being prepared; it can be changed or, if it was never submitted or revised, deleted.
  • Pending approval, then Approved or Rejected (with the approver's reason). A rejected quotation can be changed and submitted again.
  • Expired: past Valid until and not ordered. Revise it to offer it again.
  • Revised: a later revision replaces it. It stays, read-only.
  • Accepted: a Sales Order was made from it.

Revisions

Revise (on an approved, rejected or expired quotation) makes the next revision: the same number with Rev 2, Rev 3 and so on, a draft with today's date and a new Valid until. The earlier one is kept, marked Revised. Only the latest revision can become an order.

Create Sales Order

On an approved quotation, Create Sales Order asks for the cost center and the warehouse, and, if the quotation is for an opportunity, whether to mark the opportunity Won. It saves a draft Sales Order with the lines at their net prices and opens it; check and confirm it there. The quotation becomes Accepted and shows the order.

A quotation for a lead asks you to convert the lead first: it opens the lead in CRM, where Convert to Customer makes it a customer and moves its quotations to the customer.

From the rep's phone

Sales reps make quotes for their own customers and leads in the Employee Portal (see Sales reps). The price is the price list's; the rep can give a discount per line. They appear here with the rep as owner, and are approved the same way. When the customer accepts, the rep sends a draft Sales Order from the phone, which the office confirms in Sales Order.

From CRM

An opportunity or a lead in CRM has New Quotation, which opens a new quotation for it, the customer or lead and the opportunity already chosen.

Who can do what

The Quotation rights in Roles: access sees the company's quotations and prints them; write makes, submits, revises and orders them. Approving is the approval levels' (see Approval Configuration).

Common messages

  • "Only a draft or rejected quotation can be changed." Revise it instead.
  • "This quotation has expired." Change Valid until, or revise it.
  • "Convert the lead to a customer first." See Create Sales Order above.
  • "Save the quotation first." Print and Submit use what is saved.
