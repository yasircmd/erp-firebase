# Item barcodes (agreed 2026-10-09)

## The owner's request (2026-10-09)

> Currently the item code for retail sales is created by the system; how
> to use the barcode that is already on the product?

## What happens today

- An item's code is generated by the system from its category
  (`RM-000001`, `FG-000001`, ...; Item Master, ported from Code.gs) and
  can't be typed in. Items have **no barcode field**.
- The Retail Sales counter finds an item by its code, or by part of its
  code or name, typed into the item box and Enter. A USB or Bluetooth
  barcode scanner works like a keyboard (it types the digits, then
  Enter), so scanning already "types" into that box, but the product's
  barcode (e.g. EAN-13 `6291041500213`) matches no item.

## Proposed

**Barcodes on the item** (Item Master, its "write" right):
- A **Barcodes** section on an item: one or more barcodes (see
  question 64), each optionally with a pack quantity (question 65).
- A barcode is **unique across all items** (items are shared by the
  group, or restricted to one company): saving one already on another
  item is refused, naming that item.
- Letters and digits, 4 to 30 characters; an EAN-13, EAN-8 or UPC-A
  (8, 12 or 13 digits) has its check digit verified (question 66).
- **Master Data Import → Item barcodes**: Item Code, Barcode, Pack Qty,
  for loading many at once (and replacing an item's barcodes).
- Item Master's own code stays as it is (system-generated); the barcode
  is an extra way to find the item.

**At the Retail Sales counter:**
- A scan (or a barcode typed, then Enter) is looked up first as a
  barcode, then as an item code, then by name, as today. A pack barcode
  adds its pack quantity.
- An unknown barcode: "No item with barcode 6291041500213." The
  cashier can still search by name.

**Not included** (later, if wanted): weighed-item barcodes from a scale
(price or weight inside the barcode); scanning in GRN, Delivery Note or
Physical Count; printing labels (question 67).

Built as new, hand-written code (functions/src/itemBarcodes.js); Item
Master's ported save is not changed: the barcodes are stored and checked
on their own.

## Open questions

64. **Barcodes per item:**
    - **several** (the same product from two suppliers, an old and a new
      label, a pack) — recommended;
    - or one.
65. **Pack barcodes** (a carton's barcode):
    - a barcode can carry a **pack quantity** (scanning the carton of 12
      adds 12 units) — recommended;
    - or every barcode is one unit.
66. **Check digit** of EAN/UPC barcodes:
    - **refused when wrong** (catches a mistyped barcode) — recommended;
    - or accepted as typed.
67. **Items without a manufacturer's barcode** (own products, loose
    items):
    - **later**: print labels with a barcode of the item's code, as its
      own step — recommended;
    - now, with this step;
    - not needed (the cashier types or searches them).

## The owner's decisions (2026-10-09)

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 64 | Barcodes per item | **Several.** |
| 65 | Pack barcodes | **Yes**: a barcode may carry a pack quantity. |
| 66 | EAN/UPC check digit | **Refused when wrong.** |

## As built (2026-10-09)

- **Storage:** RTDB `itemBarcodes/{BARCODE}` = { ItemID, PackQty, At,
  By }, one record a barcode, so a barcode can only be on one item.
  Letters are kept in capitals (a scanner's case doesn't matter).
  functions/src/itemBarcodes.js; Item Master's ported save (codegs.js)
  is not changed.
- **Checks:** letters and digits, 4 to 30; an all-digit barcode of 8, 12,
  13 or 14 digits (EAN-8, UPC-A, EAN-13, GTIN-14) must have the right
  check digit (decision 66); a pack quantity more than 0 (empty: 1); up
  to 50 an item; a barcode on another item is refused, naming it.
- **Item Master** (screens/ITEMMASTER.html): a **Barcodes** section in
  the item form: rows of barcode and units ("Add barcode", remove); a
  scanner's Enter in a barcode box doesn't save the form. Saving saves
  the item (as before), then its barcodes (`saveItemBarcodes`, the list
  replaces the item's; Item Master's "write" right). If the barcodes are
  refused, the item is still saved and the form stays open on it with
  the reason ("The item is saved (FG-000003), but not its barcodes:
  …"); saving again doesn't make a second item. The list shows each
  item's barcodes under its name (a pack as "×12"); the search finds an
  item by its barcode. `getItemBarcodes` (Item Master's access).
- **Master Data Import → Item Barcodes** (`BARCODE`; Item Master's
  "write"): Item, Barcode, Pack Qty; each row adds a barcode. Checked
  first: the format and check digit, already on an item ("change it in
  Item Master"), twice in the sheet. An import that fails is undone.
- **Retail Sales counter:** `getRetailCounter` gives each item its
  `Barcodes`; the item box looks up a scan (or a typed barcode and
  Enter) as a barcode first, then an item code, then a name. A pack's
  barcode adds its units (decision 65). An unknown all-digit entry:
  "No item with barcode … for this company." A barcode of an item not
  sold by the company (another company's, inactive) is unknown there.

Tests: itemBarcodes suite (3), the retailSales suite's 7th, the masterImport suite and UI check (the sheet list); UI checks
itemBarcodes (4) and retailSales' 7th. Negative controls caught: no
check digit; a barcode on two items; an item's old barcodes kept; the
import not catching a barcode twice; the counter ignoring a pack's units;
the counter not looking up barcodes; a scanner's Enter saving the form;
a retry after refused barcodes making a second item. Deploy:
"functions set" `item-barcodes` with the screens; no Hosting.

**Next (decision 67, later):** labels with a barcode of the item's code
for items without one.

## Step 2 design: scanning in the stock screens (agreed 2026-10-09)

The owner (2026-10-09): GRN, and the other screens too.

### Where items are entered today

| Screen | Lines | Item entry |
|---|---|---|
| GRN, Material Issue, Stock Adjustment, Warehouse Movement | typed per line | the item code cell (type-ahead) or the search button |
| Physical Count | typed per line | the same; the screen is still generated from Code.gs (it would become hand-maintained, like Item Master) |
| Sales Order, Inventory Purchase Requisition, Production | typed per line | the same |
| Delivery Note, Purchase Return, Sales Return | taken from the source document (sales order, GRN, invoice) | no free item entry: a scan doesn't fit; not included |

### Proposed

- **The item cell accepts a barcode** on every screen above: typed or
  scanned, it is looked up as a barcode first, then as the item code (as
  today). The line gets the item; a pack's barcode sets the quantity to
  its units (question 70).
- **A Scan box above the lines** (question 71): the cursor stays there,
  each scan adds the item; scanning an item already on the document adds
  to that line's quantity instead of a new line (question 69). Made for
  receiving a delivery and counting stock with a scanner.
- An unknown barcode: "No item with barcode … for this company" (the
  line or box is left as it was).
- **Who can look barcodes up:** today only Item Master's access reads
  them. These screens' users may not have it, so a new read
  (`getBarcodeIndex`: barcode → item and units, nothing else) for any
  signed-in user; changing barcodes stays Item Master's "write".
- Shared code: one small helper
  (`window.itemBarcodes.lookup(text)`) used by every screen.

### Open questions for step 2

68. **Which screens:**
    - the stock screens: GRN, Material Issue, Stock Adjustment,
      Warehouse Movement, Physical Count — recommended;
    - and also Sales Order, Inventory Purchase Requisition, Production.
69. **Scanning an item already on the document:**
    - adds to that line's quantity (scan the same box 5 times = 5) —
      recommended;
    - or a new line each time.
70. **A pack's barcode** (a carton of 12) where the screen has units:
    - the quantity goes up by its units in the item's base unit (12
      pieces) — recommended;
    - or in the item's alternate unit when the pack matches it (1
      carton).
71. **How to scan:**
    - a Scan box above the lines, and the item cell also takes a
      barcode — recommended;
    - only the item cell (no Scan box).

## As built: step 2, scanning in the stock screens (2026-10-09)

- **Screens** (decision 68): GRN, Material Issue, Stock Adjustment,
  Warehouse Movement, Physical Count. Physical Count's screen is now
  hand-maintained (`handScreens`), like Item Master, so the Deploy
  workflow's generator leaves it as committed.
- **Scan box** (decision 71) beside "Add Line", shown while the document
  can be edited: a barcode (or an item code) and Enter. An item already
  on the document gets its line's quantity raised (decision 69); else the
  first empty line, or a new one, gets the item and the units. A carton's
  barcode adds its units in the base unit (decision 70). The box clears
  and keeps the cursor for the next scan. Physical Count raises the
  counted quantity.
- **The item cell** also takes a barcode, on Enter or leaving the cell
  (not on every key: a scanner types the digits one by one, and a part
  of a long barcode could match a short one). A carton's units fill the
  quantity when none is entered. A typed item code works as before.
- **Unknown:** "No item with barcode … ." (an all-digit entry of 8 or
  more), else "No item "…"."; nothing changes.
- **`getBarcodeIndex`** (new): barcode → item and units, for any
  signed-in user (these screens' users may not have Item Master's
  access); changing barcodes stays Item Master's "write". Each screen
  reads it once when it opens.
- Each screen has its own few lines (`barcodeItem`, `scanItem`,
  `itemCellBarcode`) rather than a shared shell helper, so no Hosting
  deploy is needed.

Tests: itemBarcodes suite's 4th (the index without Item Master's
access); UI check stockScanning (7: each screen's Scan box, GRN's item
cell, a Viewer without Item Master's access). Negative controls caught: a
repeated scan making a new line; a carton counting 1; the item cell
ignoring barcodes; an unknown barcode passing silently; the index
needing Item Master's access. Deploy: `item-barcodes-2` with the
screens.

## Step 3 design: barcodes for own products, and label printing (agreed 2026-10-09)

The owner (2026-10-09): "set barcode for manufactured product, and
printing" (decision 67's "labels later").

### Today

- Barcodes are entered or imported (step 1); nothing creates one. An own
  product (made in Production) has none unless typed in.
- Every scan already falls back to the item code (`FG-000001`): a label
  showing the item code as a Code 128 barcode would scan everywhere
  today, with no new data.
- Nothing prints barcodes; the app has no barcode-drawing library.

### Proposed

**1. Barcodes for own products** (Item Master, its "write" right):
- **Generate barcode** on an item without one, and **Generate for all
  items without a barcode** (filtered by category, e.g. Finished Goods),
  with a preview before saving.
- The number (question 72): an **EAN-13**, the retail standard any
  scanner and checkout reads:
  - with the company's **GS1 company prefix** if it has one (GS1
    membership: needed to sell own products through other retailers);
    the system adds the item reference and the check digit;
  - else an **in-store number** (EAN-13 starting 2x, reserved for use
    inside one business): fine for own shops and warehouses, not for
    selling through other retailers.
- Each number generated is unique (the same check as step 1) and saved
  as the item's barcode, so scanning works at once in Retail Sales and
  the stock screens.

**2. Printing labels** (question 73):
- **Item Master:** choose items and how many labels each.
- **Production** (a posted one): labels for what was produced, one per
  unit (changeable), with the batch and expiry when the item tracks
  them.
- **GRN** (a posted one): labels for what was received, for products
  that arrive without a barcode.

**3. The label** (questions 74, 75):
- The barcode, drawn in the browser (our own small drawing code, no
  outside library): EAN-13 for numeric barcodes, Code 128 otherwise
  (the item code when the item has no barcode);
- the item name and code; optionally the price (the company's retail
  price list) and the batch and expiry;
- printed with the browser's Print: a **label printer** roll (size set
  once, e.g. 50 × 25 mm) or **A4 sheets** of labels (columns × rows set
  once, e.g. 3 × 8). Saved per company as label settings.

### Open questions for step 3

72. **Numbers for own products:**
    - EAN-13 from the company's GS1 prefix when entered, else in-store
      numbers (2x) — recommended;
    - in-store numbers only;
    - no new numbers: print the item code as a Code 128 barcode.
73. **Where labels are printed from:**
    - Item Master, Production and GRN — recommended;
    - Item Master only.
74. **Label stock:**
    - both: a label printer roll and A4 label sheets, set per company —
      recommended;
    - label printer only;
    - A4 sheets only.
75. **On the label:**
    - name, code, barcode; the price and the batch/expiry chosen when
      printing — recommended;
    - name, code and barcode only.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 72 | Numbers for own products | **EAN-13 from the company's GS1 prefix when entered, else in-store numbers (2x).** |
| 73 | Printed from | **Item Master, Production and GRN.** |
| 74 | Label stock | **Both**: label printer roll and A4 sheets, set per company. |

## As built: step 3, barcodes for own products and labels (2026-10-09)

- **Drawing** (functions/src/barcodeSvg.js, no outside library): EAN-13
  for a 13-digit number with a right check digit, else Code 128 (set C,
  two digits a symbol, for an even run of digits; else set B). Checked
  with an independent reader (ZXing) on every EAN-13 first digit, every
  printable Code 128 set B character and every set C pair; the unit test
  pins those bit patterns. Note: an EAN-13 starting with 0 is the same
  symbol as a 12-digit UPC-A, and some scanners send it as 12 digits; our
  generated numbers never start with 0.
- **Settings per company** (`getBarcodeSettings` for any user of the
  company, `saveBarcodeSettings` with Item Master's "write"; RTDB
  `barcodeSettings/{company}`): the GS1 company prefix (6–11 digits, or
  empty), the roll label size (default 50 × 25 mm), the A4 sheet's labels
  across × down and margin (default 3 × 8, 10 mm).
- **Numbers for own products** (`generateItemBarcodes`, Item Master's
  "write"; decision 72): for items of the company (or shared) without a
  barcode; previewed (nothing kept), then saved under the lock. With a
  GS1 prefix: prefix + item reference + check digit (counter per prefix,
  `barcodeCounters/gs1/{prefix}`); without: in-store `200` + 9 digits +
  check (`barcodeCounters/instore`). A number already used (e.g. typed in)
  is skipped. Each becomes the item's one-unit barcode.
- **The label sheet** (`getLabelSheet`, any user of the company;
  decisions 74–75): per line the item's one-unit barcode (never a
  carton's), else its item code as Code 128; name and code; the price
  from the company's retail price list when asked; batch and expiry
  (dd/mm/yyyy) when asked. A roll page per label, or A4 sheets in the
  company's grid. Up to 1,000 a line, 3,000 at a time. The page is
  printed in a hidden frame, so it keeps its own label size.
- **Item Master → Barcodes & Labels** (decision 73): the company; its
  settings and the numbers (preview by category, then save) for Item
  Master's "write"; for everyone with access, labels for chosen items
  (search, labels each), roll or A4, the price option.
- **Production and GRN** (posted): **Labels**: one a unit (the base
  quantity, changeable), batch and expiry on by default, price, roll or
  A4. Production's screen is now hand-maintained (`handScreens`).

Tests: barcodeSvg unit test (3); itemBarcodes suite's 5th–7th; UI check
labelPrinting (4). Negative controls caught: a preview saving; used
numbers not skipped; a carton's barcode on a unit label; A4 paging
wrong; settings without the write right; GRN labels not following the
quantity; settings shown to a Viewer; Production's batch dropped; and
three encoding breaks (ZXing). Deploy: `item-barcodes-3` with the
screens.
