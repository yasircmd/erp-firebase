Final Settlements

For an employee on notice or who has left (a leaving date on their record). It pays everything at once: the salary of the months no posted payroll run has paid (to the leaving date, with that month's leave and inputs; the payroll run then leaves the employee out), gratuity, the annual leave balance, the accruals paid at leaving, notice pay, other additions and deductions, less every loan still owed. Submit it from the leaving date. It is approved as set up in Approval Configuration, posted, then paid on its own or in an interim payroll batch.

No one is on notice or has left without a settlement. Set the status and leaving date on the employee record first.
