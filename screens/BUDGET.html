Financial Accounting

Budget

A budget is a company's financial year: an amount per account per month, a line optionally for a cost centre or project. The first approved budget of a year is its Original; a change later is a Revision, approved again, and becomes the Current one. Every approved version is kept.

A line without a cost centre or project counts everything of its account not budgeted more finely.
