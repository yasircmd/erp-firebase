Payroll

One run per company and month, after the month's leave is locked (Leave Register → Month close). Each employee's pay comes from their compensation, the locked leave and the inputs entered for the run (overtime, bonus, commission, one-offs). How each company works out its daily rate, and where each element posts, is set in HR Setup → Payroll.
