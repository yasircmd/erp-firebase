Sales Invoice Configuration

Invoices may deliver stock directly

On: a Customer Invoice can have item lines (typed, or brought in from a Sales Order) and delivers them itself when posted, so no separate Delivery Note is needed. Off: stock leaves only with a Delivery Note, as before.

E-invoicing (UAE)

On: from its start date, each posted Customer Invoice and Sales Return (a credit note) to a customer with a TRN gets its e-invoice (PINT AE), checked for what it needs and kept with the invoice, to download. It is sent through your Accredited Service Provider once one is connected; until then nothing is sent.

Sales reps (Employee Portal)

A sales rep sees their customers in the Employee Portal (a sales manager sees all), with each one's balance; sends orders, saved here as draft Sales Orders for you to confirm, at the customer's price list; and records collections: cash as a draft Cash Receipt to the rep's cash account, a cheque as a draft PDC Received with its photo, for you to post.

Sales Types

Each Sales Type maps to its own Revenue account — Customer Invoice requires one per line, with no default, since the SAME item sold locally vs. exported often needs to post to a different account for tax reconciliation. For the same reason, each has its own Sales Returns account, which Sales Return debits for goods sold under that type.

Customer Classifications

Used by Customer Master Data — the "Export" flag determines the customer's own code prefix (CL-/CE-) and whether a Country is required. Changing a classification's flag only affects customers created afterward — never rewrites an existing customer's already-frozen code.

Links this Sales Type to one Customer Classification — e.g. "Export Sales" restricted to "Export" means it can only be used on an invoice for an Export customer. Leave unrestricted for a Sales Type that applies regardless of classification (e.g. "RM Sales", "Service Invoice").
