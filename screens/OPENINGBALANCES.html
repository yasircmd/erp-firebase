Opening Balances

A company's position on its go-live date. Set the opening date (the day before go-live) and the clearing account, then paste the GL trial balance, the stock on hand and the open invoices from Excel. Every posting goes against the clearing account; once all three are in, it should be zero. A batch posted wrongly is reversed below and posted again.

The opening date and clearing account are fixed while any batch is posted: reverse the batches to change them.
