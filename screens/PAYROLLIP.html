Interim Payroll

Pays, between the monthly payroll runs, what doesn't wait for them: posted final settlements, approved loans and advances, and the earnings (a bonus, a commission...) Earnings & Deductions marks to be paid by Interim Payroll. A batch is approved as set up in Approval Configuration ("Interim payroll"), then gives one bank file for all of it and is marked paid: each item gets its own journal, with the batch number as the reference. While in a batch, an item is paid only through it.
