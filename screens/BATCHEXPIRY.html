Click a row to open the Stock Ledger for that item and warehouse. Each batch's stock is worked out from the posted movements up to the date: an issue that names a batch is taken from that batch, otherwise from the batch that expires first (first expiry, first out). A warehouse transfer keeps the batch and its expiry. A receipt of a batch without an expiry date takes the date recorded earlier for that batch. Values are at the moving average cost the books hold, split in proportion to the quantities.
