The parts of an employee's pay (the first piece of Payroll). Fixed elements are monthly amounts on the employee (basic, allowances, fixed deductions); variable ones are agreed terms (bonus, commission), paid per payroll run. One fixed earning is the Basic: the grade's pay band is checked against it.

How each company's payroll works it out, and where each pay element posts. The daily rate prorates pay for joiners, leavers and unpaid days, and pays leave at basic or a %. Each element posts to its GL account by the employee's cost center (a company may use its own account); the lines are clubbed as for inventory.
