The parts of an employee's pay (the first piece of Payroll). Fixed elements are monthly amounts on the employee (basic, allowances, fixed deductions); variable ones are agreed terms (bonus, commission), paid per payroll run. One fixed earning is the Basic: the grade's pay band is checked against it.

How each company's payroll works it out, and where each pay element posts. The daily rate prorates pay for joiners, leavers and unpaid days, and pays leave at basic or a %. Each element posts to its GL account by the employee's cost center (a company may use its own account); the lines are clubbed as for inventory.

Requests from the Employee Portal (all optional)

Why employees leave. An employee on notice or who has left has one of these (Employees → Leaving reason), with a note for any detail. Each reason is a resignation, a termination or other. A reason no longer in use stays on the employees who have it.

The kinds of expenses employees claim in the Employee Portal. An approved claim is paid with the next payroll as an earning, "Reimbursement — <category>" (a pay element each category keeps for itself), posted to the category's expense account (the group's, or a company's own). A claim over the monthly limit is flagged to the approvers, who decide.
