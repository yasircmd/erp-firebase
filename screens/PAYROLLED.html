Earnings & Deductions

Overtime (hours for elements paid by the hour, else an amount), bonus, commission and one-off additions or deductions for a month, each against a variable pay element, with their supporting documents. Enter them before the run: the month's run takes them when it is calculated and posts them. They can change until the run is submitted. An earning by amount can instead be paid by Interim Payroll.

One row a line: employee no., element code, hours or amount, note (and "Interim" in a fifth column to pay it by Interim Payroll).
