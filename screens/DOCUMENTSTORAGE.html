Document Storage

Where the supporting documents attached to entries are kept. The ERP signs in to Google Drive as the account connected here, with permission only for the files and folders it creates itself, and keeps them in a private "ERP Documents" folder: company / year / voucher type / document number. Users never get the folder: they open the documents through the ERP, on the entry. Only Admin sees this screen.
