Material Variance Report

Covers every item produced in the period, not one product at a time. Each unit is judged against whichever BOM was actually in effect on the date it was produced — if a recipe changed mid-period, both versions are correctly accounted for in their own proportion, not blended incorrectly.

"Actual" consumption nets Material Issue, Stock Adjustment, and Physical Count together for each material — not Material Issue alone — since all three move real material out of (or back into) inventory.
