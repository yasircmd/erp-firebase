Groups

Categories of client corporates. A module that came after the groups (a later module) is off for every client until it is provided to their group here, or ticked for one corporate in its Modules.

Exchange-rates sheet

One Google Sheet for every corporate, columns Currency, Base and Rate (e.g. USD, AED, =GOOGLEFINANCE("CURRENCY:USDAED")), one row per pair a client uses. Publish it to the web as CSV and paste its link. Each corporate reads it in Currency Configuration and applies the rates it confirms.
