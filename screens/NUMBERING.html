Numbering

Each document number and code is built from its parts: the prefix, the company code, the year and the running number. The defaults are the system's own. A change applies to new numbers only; with the year in the format the number starts again each year, without the company code all companies share one sequence. The samples show company 01 and this year.

You can see the numbering but not change it.
