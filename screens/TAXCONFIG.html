Tax Configuration

The company's sales tax: its regime (UAE VAT, India GST or its own), the components it is charged as, each with its output and input account, and its tax codes with their rates by date. An item takes its tax code in Item Master; a customer or vendor its registration type, state and tax number. A document takes the rate valid on its date, so a change of rate is a new row with the date it applies from.

Tax codes

A code's rate is the one valid on the document's date. GST: within the company's state the rate is charged as CGST and SGST (UTGST in a union territory), half each; to another state, an SEZ or abroad as IGST. Recoverable: how much of the input tax on a purchase may be reclaimed (the rest is part of the cost).
