The parts of an employee's pay (the first piece of Payroll). Fixed elements are monthly amounts on the employee (basic, allowances, fixed deductions); variable ones are agreed terms (bonus, commission), paid per payroll run. One fixed earning is the Basic: the grade's pay band is checked against it.

The kinds of leave, with their rules. Leave that uses a balance earns days by grade level and years of service, accrued monthly over the calendar year; unused days carry forward up to a cap and the rest lapses. Leave pay rules say how each day is paid, by grade level and by the days of that leave already taken in the year (e.g. sick leave: 15 days full, 30 at half pay, then unpaid). One set for the group. Duty (e.g. business travel) is paid as working.
