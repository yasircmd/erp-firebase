## What it is for

**Capital Work in Progress** tracks a project or asset under construction before it is fully ready for use. This keeps the asset value segregated until it is ready for capitalization.

## Before you start

- The project or asset class is configured.
- The asset under construction is assigned to an accountable class.
- The user knows which costs belong in the capital work account.

## Step by step

1. Open **Capital Work in Progress** and select the company.
2. Add the work-in-progress asset or project.
3. Record the values or costs being accumulated.
4. When the asset is ready, transfer it to the final fixed asset record.

## What happens in the books

The balance remains in the capital work in progress account until the asset is transferred into service and capitalized.

## Rights

Finance and asset setup roles generally maintain this area.

## Questions and mistakes

- If the transfer is not visible, check that the work-in-progress asset is active and linked to the correct class.
