## What it is for

**HR Setup** is the foundation for the employee and payroll structure. It keeps the company’s HR defaults and rule base consistent across employee records, leave and payroll processing.

## Before you start

- The company, legal entity and departments are already set up.
- The payroll and leave policy is agreed by the business.
- The managing team knows which default earnings and deductions are used.

## Step by step

1. Open **HR Setup** and select the company.
2. Review the default employee, leave and payroll settings.
3. Confirm company-specific rules and the linking to payroll accounts.
4. Save and test the setup with a sample employee or pay run.

## What happens in the books

HR Setup is configuration rather than a posting document. It determines the rules that payroll, leave and employee administration follow later.

## Rights

Typically restricted to HR, payroll and admin roles with setup access.

## Questions and mistakes

- A missing payroll link or company default can block pay processing or leave approvals.
- If a rule does not appear to apply, check that the employee’s company and the setup are aligned.
