## What it is for

**Leave** records employee leave requests, approvals and final leave balances. It determines what the employee has taken and what remains available.

## Before you start

- The employee and line manager are set up.
- Leave types and accrual rules exist in the company setup.
- The leave date range is known.

## Step by step

1. Open **Leave** and choose the employee.
2. Add the leave type, period and dates.
3. Submit it for approval if the company requires manager approval.
4. Review the updated leave balance after approval.

## What happens in the books

Leave is mainly a payroll and attendance control document. It affects the employee’s available balance and any salary deduction if the leave is unpaid or partial.

## Rights

Employees, managers and HR/payroll roles need the correct rights to apply, approve and track leave.

## Questions and mistakes

- If the leave is not approved, check whether the request is still pending on the manager.
- If the balance appears wrong, the employee’s accrual or previous leave posting may not be configured correctly.
