## What it is for

**Organisation Units** defines the company’s hierarchy, departments and operating units that other modules use for reporting and approval.

## Before you start

- The company structure is known and agreed.
- Departments or cost centers are mapped to reporting needs.

## Step by step

1. Open **Organisation Units** and select the company.
2. Add or update the departments or divisions needed.
3. Review how each unit maps to the relevant cost center or manager assignments.
4. Save and use it in a test posting or approval.

## Rights

Admin and finance system setup roles usually manage organisation units.

## Questions and mistakes

- If a cost center or department is missing in a reporting screen, check whether the unit is active and linked to the correct company.
