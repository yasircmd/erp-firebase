## What it is for

**Asset Register** holds the list of active assets with their carrying values, depreciation history and status for the selected company.

## Before you start

- Assets have been capitalized and entered into the register.
- The company and period are known.

## Step by step

1. Open **Asset Register** and filter by asset class or department if needed.
2. Review the current carrying value, depreciation and life status.
3. Use the report for audits, finance review or asset management follow-up.

## What happens in the books

This is a reporting screen. It reads the asset register and does not itself create entries.

## Rights

Read access is normally enough for most users; finance and auditors often need additional access.

## Questions and mistakes

- If an asset is missing, check whether it was entered and whether the asset was capitalised in the correct company.
