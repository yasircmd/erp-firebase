## What it is for

**Leave Reports** summarises leave by employee, department and period. It helps managers review balances and spot outstanding or over-allowed leave.

## Before you start

- The leave types are configured for the company.
- Employees have a valid manager or department assignment.
- The reporting period is known.

## Step by step

1. Open **Leave Reports** and choose the company or department.
2. Filter by period or employee range.
3. Review balances and leave status.
4. Use the report for manager follow-up and payroll planning.

## What happens in the books

This is a reporting view only. It reads leave balances and requests without posting to the ledger.

## Rights

Manager and HR reporting access is commonly enough.

## Questions and mistakes

- If balances appear missing, check whether the employee record and leave transactions were posted in the same company.
