## What it is for

**Inventory Valuation** is the wider stock value view for the selected warehouse and period. Use it for valuation balance checking and review.

## Before you start

- The stock ledger is complete and posted.
- Warehouse and item filters are known.

## Step by step

1. Open **Inventory Valuation**.
2. Choose the company and warehouse or item range.
3. Run the report and review the inventory value.

## What happens in the books

This report reads stock value and cost information. It does not create entries.

## Rights

Report access is usually enough.

## Questions and mistakes

- If the valuation is materially off, review recent GRNs, issues and count adjustments.
