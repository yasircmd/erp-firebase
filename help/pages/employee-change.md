## What it is for

**Employee Change** records changes in an employee’s status or assignment, such as a move in department, pay changes or change in manager.

## Before you start

- The employee record exists.
- The new role, manager or pay details are agreed.
- The effective date is known.

## Step by step

1. Open **Employee Change** and select the employee.
2. Enter the change and the effective date.
3. Review the impact on pay, leave or role assignment.
4. Save and confirm the updated record is visible in payroll and leave screens.

## What happens in the books

The change itself is mostly a control record, but the payroll run later reads it to determine the employee’s effective cost and entitlement.

## Rights

HR officers and payroll admins typically need access to employee changes.

## Questions and mistakes

- If the new pay or role is not used, check whether the effective date is after the current payroll run.
- If the update is rejected, confirm the employee and company details are active.
