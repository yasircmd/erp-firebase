## What it is for

**Bill of Materials** lists the materials, quantities and substitutions needed to build a finished item or product.

## Before you start

- The item master is complete for the finished product and the components.
- The company knows the standard recipe and any allowable substitutions.
- The production policy is agreed to maintain a standard BOM.

## Step by step

1. Open **Bill of Materials** and select the parent item.
2. Add or update the materials and their required quantities.
3. Save the BOM and check it against actual production experience.

## What happens in the books

The BOM itself does not post. It is the recipe used during the production and consumption process.

## Rights

Production planning or admin setup users usually maintain BOMs.

## Questions and mistakes

- If a production job is short of materials, review the BOM and whether the right version is active.
