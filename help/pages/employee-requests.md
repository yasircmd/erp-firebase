## What it is for

**Employee Requests** handles employee queries, reimbursement claims, leave changes and other tasks routed to the relevant manager or HR team.

## Before you start

- The employee and line manager are in place.
- The requests are linked to the correct company and category.
- If the portal is used, employee access is already configured.

## Step by step

1. Open **Employee Requests** and choose the company or employee.
2. Review the pending request, case notes and any file attachments.
3. Assign the request to the right handler or approver.
4. Resolve the request and confirm the final status.

## What happens in the books

This screen is mainly a workflow control. Some requests may feed payroll or reimbursement processing, but the request itself remains a workflow record unless linked to a financial posting.

## Rights

Line managers, HR staff and payroll administrators typically need rights to review and close employee requests.

## Questions and mistakes

- If a request is not visible, check whether the employee is in the same company and whether the request is assigned to the correct approval flow.
