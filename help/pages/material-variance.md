## What it is for

**Material Variance** flags differences between the standard material expected for a production job and the actual material consumed.

## Before you start

- The production job is posted or partially complete.
- The standard BOM is available for comparison.
- The actual material usage is known and checked.

## Step by step

1. Open **Material Variance** and select the production job or item.
2. Compare the planned and actual consumption figures.
3. Review the variance by material and reason.
4. Record the difference if the company expects a variance analysis or account posting.

## What happens in the books

The variance helps explain booking differences in production cost and stock. It is often used to identify wastage, scrap or overuse of materials.

## Rights

Production and finance staff often use this screen for variance review and sign-off.

## Questions and mistakes

- If the variance is large, check whether the BOM is outdated or the actual consumption data is incomplete.
