## What it is for

**Provisions** calculates and maintains liabilities for accrued employee costs such as leave provisions, gratuity or other long-term payroll commitments.

## Before you start

- The company’s provision policy is agreed.
- The payroll elements and GL accounts for provision entries are configured.
- The employees and balances are active in the payroll records.

## Step by step

1. Open **Provisions** and select the company.
2. Review the employee provision balances and the relevant accrual basis.
3. Update the provision entry or calculation if a company policy changes.
4. Post or review the approved amounts and the GL impact.

## What happens in the books

The provisions create or adjust the company’s accrued payroll liabilities. These amounts are then used to support financial reporting and payroll planning.

## Rights

Usually limited to payroll and finance roles with provision access.

## Questions and mistakes

- An incorrect provision usually comes from a missing company policy or an employee record that was not updated.
