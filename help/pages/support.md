## What it is for

**Support** is where your Admin tells the ERP's support team about a
problem, a question or a change you need, and follows their answers. Only
users given Support in Roles (by default, Admin) see it.

**What you send is shared.** The text and the files go to the support
team and are kept in their account, outside your organisation's own. The
screen asks you to confirm this every time you send. Send only what the
issue needs: one entry's screenshot, not a whole report.

## Step by step

1. Open **Support** and select **New issue**.
2. Choose what it is (**Bug**, **Question** or **Change request**), give it
   a short title (for example "The invoice total shows 0"), and pick the
   screen it is about.
3. Say what you did, what you expected and what you saw instead. Attach up
   to 5 files of 10 MB each if they help: screenshots, PDF, Word or Excel.
4. Select **Send to support** and confirm. The issue gets a number, such
   as 200-00001.
5. Open an issue from the list to read the team's answers. **Waiting for
   you** means they need something from you: write your answer (with files
   if needed) and **Send answer**.
6. When it is sorted, the team marks it **Resolved**. Select **Close the
   issue** when you are done with it; for anything more, send a new one.

## Statuses

- **New:** sent, not yet picked up.
- **In progress:** the support team is on it.
- **Waiting for you:** they asked you something.
- **Resolved:** they consider it done; answering reopens it.
- **Closed:** finished. It stays in the list to read.
