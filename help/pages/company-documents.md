## What it is for

**Company Documents** maintains the documents and compliance records linked to the company, including licences, certifications or supporting files that need periodic review.

## Before you start

- The company and document type are known.
- The expiry date or review cycle is agreed.

## Step by step

1. Open **Company Documents** and select the company.
2. Add or update the document record and expiry dates.
3. Attach the file if needed.
4. Save and review whether the record is close to expiry or overdue.

## What happens in the books

This screen mostly maintains records and reminders. It does not create ledger postings by itself.

## Rights

Admin, company admin and compliance or HR users typically maintain these records.

## Questions and mistakes

- If an expiry or alert is missing, check the document type and the date configuration on the record.
