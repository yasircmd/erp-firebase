## What it is for

| Statement | Shows | Period |
|---|---|---|
| **Profit and Loss (P&L)** | Revenue, cost of sales and expenses, and the profit | From – To |
| **Balance Sheet** | Assets, liabilities and equity | As of a date |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Cash in and out, by Operating, Investing and Financing | From – To |

## Step by step

1. Open the statement and choose the **Company**.
2. Choose the period (**From** and **To**) or, for the Balance Sheet, the
   **As Of** date.
3. Narrow by **Division**, **Branch**, **Cost Center** or **Profit Center**
   if needed (for example a P&L per cost center).
4. Select **Run Report**.
5. Select an account line to open its
   [General Ledger](general-ledger.html); **Back** returns.
6. **Copy Table** to Excel, or **Print**.

## Good to know

- The **P&L** leaves out the year-end closing entry, so a closed year
  still shows its profit.
- The **Cash Flow** is by the direct method: each cash or bank movement is
  classed by the account on its other side. That account's **Cash Flow
  Category** comes from its header in the
  [Chart of Accounts](chart-of-accounts.html); without one it counts as
  Operating.
- The figures follow the posted entries only; drafts are not included.
